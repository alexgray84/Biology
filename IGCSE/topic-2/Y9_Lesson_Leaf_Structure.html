5

The 8 layers of a leaf

15 min

This is the big one. Look at the diagram, then for each layer write down (a) what it is and (b) one job it does.

✍️ Annotate the diagram 📋 List 8 bullets 🗣️ Teach a partner
Notes for each layer (in order)
  1. Waxy cuticle — waterproof, stops water loss.
  2. Upper epidermis — thin & transparent, lets light through.
  3. Palisade mesophyll — packed columns, full of chloroplasts → main photosynthesis.
  4. Spongy mesophyll — round cells with air gaps → gas exchange.
  5. Vascular bundle — contains xylem (water) + phloem (sugar).
  6. Lower epidermis — bottom skin, has the stomata.
  7. Stomata — tiny pores, gases in and out, water vapour out.
  8. Guard cells — open and close the stomata.
Sentence frame (re-use for each layer)

"The ____ is the layer that ____. It is built for this because ____."

Push yourself — explain, don't just state
  • Why is the waxy cuticle important on a sunny day?
  • Why are palisade cells column-shaped and packed at the top?
  • Why are stomata mostly on the bottom of the leaf, not the top?

7

Transport tubes — Xylem vs Phloem

5 min

Make a comparison table in your book with three columns: Feature · Xylem · Phloem. Use the info below.

XYLEM — DEAD pipes 💀
  • Dead, hollow cells
  • Thick, strong walls (lignin)
  • Carries water + minerals
  • One direction: roots → leaves
PHLOEM — LIVING tubes 🌱
  • Living cells in columns
  • Carries sucrose (sugar)
  • Both directions — wherever needed
  • Sieve plates between cells
Compare-and-contrast stem

"Both xylem and phloem ____, but xylem ____ whereas phloem ____."

Stretch — exam style

"Explain how the structure of the leaf is adapted for efficient photosynthesis." (6 marks)

Plan three points before you write: 1) light, 2) gases, 3) water. One point about each.

8

Apply — Exam questions

7 min

Have a go at the questions on your printed sheet. Aim for the marks shown — 1 mark = 1 point you need to make.

How to read the question
  • State / Name → just write the answer (1 word is fine).
  • Describe → say what happens / what you see, no reasons needed.
  • Explain → say why. Use "because…" or "this means that…".
  • Always check the marks — that's how many points you need.
Answer starters
  • "Plants need stomata so that ____."
  • "The root hair cell is adapted because ____, which means ____."
  • "When the stomata close, the plant ____, which is useful because ____."

Key Words

Stuck on a word? Check here any time.

Cell
Smallest unit of a living thing.
Tissue
A group of similar cells working together.
Organ
Group of tissues with one job (e.g. leaf).
Cuticle
Waxy waterproof outer layer.
Epidermis
Thin protective skin of the leaf.
Palisade mesophyll
Column cells full of chloroplasts — most photosynthesis.
Spongy mesophyll
Round cells with air spaces — gas exchange.
Xylem
Dead tubes — water + minerals up.
Phloem
Living tubes — sugar both ways.
Stoma / Stomata
Tiny pore for gas exchange.
Guard cell
Bean-shaped cell that opens / closes a stoma.
Chloroplast
Where photosynthesis happens — green from chlorophyll.
Diffusion
Movement of gases from high → low concentration.
