Build the equation — word & symbol

8 min

In your book, write the word equation first. Then have a go at the balanced symbol equation. Check using the panels.

carbon dioxide  +  water   sunlight ⟶ chlorophyll   glucose  +  oxygen
6CO2  +  6H2O   sunlight ⟶ chlorophyll   C6H12O6  +  6O2
✍️ Copy into book 🧠 Memorise the symbols
What does "balanced" mean?

Atoms can't be created or destroyed. So the same number of each atom must appear on both sides.

Count carbons: 6 on the left (in 6CO₂) and 6 on the right (in C₆H₁₂O₆). ✓
Count hydrogens: 12 on the left (in 6H₂O) and 12 on the right. ✓
Count oxygens: 12 + 6 = 18 on the left, 6 + 12 = 18 on the right. ✓

How to read the question
  • State / Give → just write the answer.
  • Explain → say WHY. Use the words "because…" or "this means…".
  • Balance → make atoms equal on both sides.
Answer starters
  • "Guard cells help photosynthesis because they ____, which lets ____ enter the leaf."
  • "Palisade cells help photosynthesis because they contain ____ which absorb ____."
  • "Three uses of glucose are ____, ____ and ____."
Mark scheme hints
  • (a) 6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂.
  • (b) Light · chlorophyll.
  • Guard cells: open the stomata so CO₂ can diffuse in (and O₂ out).
  • Palisade cells: column shape + many chloroplasts at the top to absorb maximum light.
  • Glucose uses: respiration (energy), starch storage, cellulose for cell walls, making proteins (with N), making chlorophyll (with Mg). Any 3.

Key Words

Stuck on a word? Check here any time.

Photosynthesis
Reaction in leaves where plants make glucose using sunlight.
Chloroplast
Where photosynthesis happens — found in palisade cells.
Chlorophyll
Green pigment that absorbs sunlight.
Carbon dioxide (CO₂)
Reactant — enters via stomata.
Water (H₂O)
Reactant — comes up the xylem from roots.
Glucose
The sugar made — formula C₆H₁₂O₆.
Oxygen (O₂)
Product (by-product) — leaves via stomata.
Starch
Insoluble store of glucose for later.
Cellulose
Tough fibres that build plant cell walls.
Synthesis
Combining glucose with minerals to make new molecules (proteins, chlorophyll).
Respiration
All living cells release energy from glucose.
Stomata
Pores on the underside of the leaf — gas exchange.
Diffusion
Particles spread from high → low concentration.
Reactant
Goes IN to the reaction.
Product
Comes OUT of the reaction.
