# KS3 Energy quiz, question set v1

For review before build. Authored in SATs style using current energy stores and transfers language, not the dated 2003 to 2009 "forms of energy" wording.

## Structure

- 30 questions, 1 mark each, total 30
- Five topics: stores (7), transfers and pathways (6), resources (6), efficiency (6), power and units (5)
- Level split: 15 at Tier 3-6, 15 at Tier 5-7, ramping within each topic
- All questions are auto-markable: 26 multiple choice, 4 numeric. No free-text needed, so the self-marking logic stays clean
- Heat transfer deliberately excluded as requested

Creative decisions worth a sanity check: I have used the stores and pathways model your students are taught, included a few misconception probes (light as a "store", nuclear as "renewable"), and kept the maths to clean whole numbers.

## Topic 1: Energy stores (Q1 to Q7)

**Q1 · 3-6 · MCQ**
A moving football has energy in which store?
- A) thermal
- B) kinetic
- C) chemical
- D) elastic

Answer: B

**Q2 · 3-6 · MCQ**
A battery stores energy mainly in which store?
- A) kinetic
- B) gravitational potential
- C) chemical
- D) elastic

Answer: C

**Q3 · 3-6 · MCQ**
A book rests on a shelf. Which change increases the energy in its gravitational potential store the most?
- A) moving the book to a lower shelf
- B) moving the book to a higher shelf
- C) opening the book
- D) cooling the book

Answer: B

**Q4 · 5-7 · MCQ**
A stretched elastic band is released and fires across the room. Which describes the main energy change?
- A) kinetic store to elastic store
- B) elastic store to kinetic store
- C) chemical store to thermal store
- D) thermal store to kinetic store

Answer: B

**Q5 · 5-7 · MCQ**
Which of these is NOT an energy store?
- A) chemical
- B) kinetic
- C) light
- D) gravitational potential

Answer: C (light is a transfer pathway by radiation, not a store)

**Q6 · 5-7 · MCQ**
As a roller coaster car speeds down the first hill, the main energy transfer is from the:
- A) kinetic store to the gravitational potential store
- B) gravitational potential store to the kinetic store
- C) chemical store to the elastic store
- D) thermal store to the kinetic store

Answer: B

**Q7 · 5-7 · MCQ**
Two balls are lifted to the same height. Ball X has a greater mass than ball Y. Which statement is correct?
- A) Ball X has more energy in its gravitational potential store
- B) Ball Y has more energy in its gravitational potential store
- C) They have equal gravitational potential stores
- D) Neither ball has a gravitational potential store

Answer: A

## Topic 2: Energy transfers and pathways (Q8 to Q13)

**Q8 · 3-6 · MCQ**
When a torch is switched on, energy leaves the chemical store of the battery. Which pathway carries it to the bulb?
- A) by heating
- B) electrically
- C) mechanically
- D) by radiation

Answer: B

**Q9 · 3-6 · MCQ**
A chef pushes a heavy trolley across a kitchen. Energy is transferred:
- A) electrically
- B) by radiation
- C) mechanically
- D) by heating

Answer: C

**Q10 · 3-6 · MCQ**
The Sun warms your face on a sunny day. Energy from the Sun reaches you mainly:
- A) by heating the air
- B) mechanically
- C) electrically
- D) by radiation

Answer: D

**Q11 · 5-7 · MCQ**
Which sequence best describes a kettle heating water?
- A) thermal store, transferred electrically, to a kinetic store
- B) mains supply, transferred electrically, raising the thermal store of the water
- C) chemical store of the water, transferred by heating, to a kinetic store
- D) gravitational store, transferred mechanically, to a thermal store

Answer: B

**Q12 · 5-7 · MCQ**
A wind-up radio is wound by hand, then plays music. While the handle is being turned, the energy pathway is:
- A) electrically
- B) mechanically
- C) by radiation
- D) by heating

Answer: B

**Q13 · 5-7 · MCQ**
Which statement about energy transfer is correct?
- A) Energy can be transferred but the total amount is always conserved
- B) Energy is used up and disappears when a device is switched on
- C) Energy can be created by an electric motor
- D) Energy is destroyed when it becomes heat

Answer: A

## Topic 3: Energy resources (Q14 to Q19)

**Q14 · 3-6 · MCQ**
Which of these is a fossil fuel?
- A) wind
- B) coal
- C) sunlight
- D) hydroelectricity

Answer: B

**Q15 · 3-6 · MCQ**
Which energy resource is renewable?
- A) oil
- B) coal
- C) wind
- D) natural gas

Answer: C

**Q16 · 3-6 · MCQ**
Fossil fuels were formed from:
- A) melted rocks
- B) the remains of dead organisms over millions of years
- C) water and sunlight
- D) recycled plastic

Answer: B

**Q17 · 5-7 · MCQ**
Which is a disadvantage of burning fossil fuels?
- A) they are renewable
- B) they release carbon dioxide, a greenhouse gas
- C) they never run out
- D) they produce no waste

Answer: B

**Q18 · 5-7 · MCQ**
A student says, "Nuclear power is renewable because there is lots of uranium." Which is the best response?
- A) correct, uranium is renewable
- B) incorrect, nuclear fuel is non-renewable because the uranium is used up and not replaced
- C) correct, nuclear power uses sunlight
- D) incorrect, nuclear power is a fossil fuel

Answer: B

**Q19 · 5-7 · MCQ**
Which renewable resource depends on the gravitational pull of the Moon?
- A) solar
- B) wind
- C) tidal
- D) geothermal

Answer: C

## Topic 4: Efficiency (Q20 to Q25)

**Q20 · 3-6 · MCQ**
In every energy transfer, some energy spreads out to the surroundings, usually to the:
- A) chemical store
- B) thermal store
- C) elastic store
- D) nuclear store

Answer: B

**Q21 · 3-6 · MCQ**
An old filament lamp gives out light but also gets very hot. The energy that heats the room is:
- A) useful energy
- B) wasted (dissipated) energy
- C) stored energy
- D) created energy

Answer: B

**Q22 · 3-6 · Numeric**
A device takes in 100 J of energy and usefully transfers 80 J. How much energy is wasted? Give your answer in joules.

Answer: 20 (J)

**Q23 · 5-7 · Numeric**
A motor is supplied with 200 J electrically and usefully transfers 150 J to a load. Calculate its efficiency as a percentage.

Answer: 75 (%)

**Q24 · 5-7 · MCQ**
A Sankey diagram shows 60 J going in and 24 J usefully transferred out. The efficiency is:
- A) 24%
- B) 40%
- C) 60%
- D) 84%

Answer: B

**Q25 · 5-7 · MCQ**
Why can no real device ever be 100% efficient?
- A) because energy is destroyed
- B) because some energy is always dissipated to the surroundings, usually as thermal energy
- C) because energy is created
- D) because devices are switched off

Answer: B

## Topic 5: Power and units (Q26 to Q30)

**Q26 · 3-6 · MCQ**
What is the unit of energy?
- A) watt
- B) joule
- C) newton
- D) volt

Answer: B

**Q27 · 3-6 · MCQ**
What is the unit of power?
- A) joule
- B) watt
- C) ampere
- D) metre

Answer: B

**Q28 · 3-6 · MCQ**
A 60 W bulb and a 100 W bulb are compared. Which transfers energy faster?
- A) the 60 W bulb
- B) the 100 W bulb
- C) they transfer at the same rate
- D) neither transfers energy

Answer: B

**Q29 · 5-7 · Numeric**
A lamp transfers 600 J of energy in 30 s. Calculate its power in watts. (power = energy / time)

Answer: 20 (W)

**Q30 · 5-7 · Numeric**
A kettle has a power of 2000 W. How much energy does it transfer in 60 s? Give your answer in joules. (energy = power x time)

Answer: 120000 (J)
