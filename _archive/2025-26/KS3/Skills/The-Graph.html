Selection: Line vs. Bar

Select the correct graph for these data types. (Hover to see the logic).

Rule A Continuous Data

Numbers that come from measurements (Time, Temp, Length). Use a Line Graph.

Rule B Discrete Data

Words, groups, or "counting" numbers (Colors, Types, Names). Use a Bar Chart.

Experiment: Favourite Social Media Apps Bar Chart (Categorical)
Experiment: Temperature of cooling tea every 1 min Line Graph (Continuous)
