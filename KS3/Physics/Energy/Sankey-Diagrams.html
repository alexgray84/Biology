The Anatomy of a Sankey Diagram

8 min

Examine the Sankey diagram for a lightbulb below. Copy the three golden rules into your book.

INPUT
100 J
USEFUL (Light) 20 J ➔
WASTED (Heat) 80 J ▼
The 3 Golden Rules:
  1. The Input energy goes in on the left.
  2. Useful energy continues straight on to the right.
  3. Wasted energy curves downwards.
⚡ Push yourself

If the input arrow is 10 squares wide, and the useful arrow is 2 squares wide, how many squares wide must the wasted arrow be? Why?

🔐 Riddle to unlock

"The most important rule of a Sankey diagram is that the _____ of the arrow represents the amount of energy." (5 letters)

✓ Answers

The WIDTH of the arrow shows the amount of energy.

Stretch answer: The wasted arrow must be 8 squares wide, because energy cannot be destroyed. Input (10) = Useful (2) + Wasted (8).

Interpreting Data: The TV

10 min

A TV uses 200J of electrical energy. It converts 60J into light energy, 40J into sound energy, and 100J into heat energy. Answer the questions in your book.

  1. What is the total Input energy?
  2. What are the two useful energy output stores?
  3. What is the wasted energy store, and why is it wasted?
  4. If you drew this on graph paper where 1 square = 20J, how many squares wide would the Input arrow be?
💡 Hint

For question 3: Think about the purpose of a TV. Do you buy a TV to warm up your living room?

For question 4: Divide the total input (200) by the scale (20).

📚 Sentence stems
  • "The two useful energy stores for a TV are _____ and _____."
  • "Heat is wasted energy for a TV because the purpose of a TV is to _____, not to _____."
🔐 Riddle to unlock

"The arrow pointing downwards on a Sankey diagram always represents ______ energy." (6 letters)

✓ Answers
  1. Total Input: 200J.
  2. Useful stores: Light and Sound.
  3. Wasted store: Heat (Thermal). It is wasted because a TV is designed to be watched and listened to, not used as a heater.
  4. Input arrow width: 200 ÷ 20 = 10 squares wide.

💡 Hint

Divide the energy value in Joules by 50 to find the number of squares.

🔐 Riddle to unlock

"To draw these diagrams accurately on graph paper, you must establish a _____, like 1 square = 10J." (5 letters)

✓ Answers

For the Blender at a scale of 1 square = 50J:

  • Input (500J): 10 squares
  • Useful Kinetic (50J): 1 square
  • Wasted Heat (150J): 3 squares
  • Wasted Sound (300J): 6 squares

Check: 1 + 3 + 6 = 10 squares! The input matches the outputs.

Calculating Efficiency

8 min

Use the formula below to calculate the efficiency of the devices from the worksheet. Show your working in your book.

Efficiency = (Useful Energy Out ÷ Total Energy In) × 100%
  1. A Blender converts 700J of input energy into 150J of useful energy.
  2. A Hairdryer has a total input of 500J and a useful output of 200J.
📚 Sentence stems
  • "Blender Efficiency = (150 ÷ _____) × 100 = _____ %"
  • "Hairdryer Efficiency = (_____ ÷ 500) × 100 = _____ %"
🔐 Riddle to unlock

"The math formula (Useful Out ÷ Total In) helps us calculate the __________ of a device." (10 letters)

✓ Answers
  1. Blender: (150 ÷ 700) × 100 = 21.4% efficient.
  2. Hairdryer: (200 ÷ 500) × 100 = 40% efficient.

Exam Question [6 marks]: Compare the advantages and disadvantages of buying compact fluorescent bulbs (CFLs) rather than old filament bulbs.

💡 Hint

Look at the input energy. The CFL uses much less input energy to produce the exactly same amount of light (0.8J). What does this mean for the user's electricity bill?
However, CFLs contain toxic mercury vapour making them hard to throw away.

🔐 Final riddle to unlock

"CFL stands for Compact ___________ Lightbulb." (11 letters)

✓ Answers & Mark Scheme

Advantages of CFLs (up to 3 marks):

  • They are much more efficient (4x more efficient).
  • They are cheaper to use because they require less input energy.
  • They are better for the environment (less fossil fuels burned).
  • They last much longer than filament bulbs.

Disadvantages of CFLs (up to 3 marks):

  • They are more expensive to buy initially.
  • Disposal is a problem because they contain toxic mercury vapour.
  • Their lifespan is shortened if turned on and off very frequently.

Key Terms

📉
Sankey Diagram
A visual way to represent energy transfers where the width of the arrow shows the amount of energy.
➡️
Useful Energy
The energy transferred for the intended purpose of the device (straight arrow).
⬇️
Wasted Energy
Energy dissipated to the surroundings, usually as heat (downward arrow).
📏
Scale
A ratio used to draw diagrams accurately, e.g., 1 square on graph paper = 10 Joules.
🧮
Efficiency
(Useful Energy Out ÷ Total Energy In) × 100%. A measure of how little energy a device wastes.
