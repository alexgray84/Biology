Energy

Stores, transfers & efficiency

What you'll learn in this topic

  • The eight energy stores: kinetic, gravitational, chemical, elastic, thermal, magnetic, electrostatic and nuclear
  • How energy is transferred between stores by mechanical work, heating, electricity and radiation
  • The principle of conservation of energy
  • How to calculate efficiency and represent transfers using Sankey diagrams
  • How to identify wasted energy and ways to reduce it
