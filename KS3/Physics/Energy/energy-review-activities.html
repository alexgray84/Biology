Group Review Activities

Six activities to review the whole Energy topic — 6–10 minutes each

👥 How to use these activities

Work in your table group. Each activity has a timer and a shared challenge. Discuss your answers before revealing — everyone should be able to explain the answer, not just one person. Your teacher will choose which activity to run.

⚡
Energy Store Blitz
Energy Stores
A scenario appears — your group names the energy store. Six rounds, quick-fire, and compare with other groups.
⏱ 6 min 👥 Group
🔗
Transfer Chain Builder
Energy Transfers
Build the energy transfer sequence for three different scenarios. Drag-and-drop the right stores in the right order.
⏱ 8 min 👥 Group
📊
Sankey Showdown
Energy Efficiency
Estimate the efficiency of eight everyday devices and energy sources, then see the Sankey diagram and the efficiency calculation for each.
⏱ 12 min 👥 Group
♻️
Renewables Sort
Energy Resources
Sort ten energy sources into renewable and non-renewable. Race against the clock and check your score.
⏱ 6 min 👥 Group
🍎
Food & Fuels Fast Round
Food & Fuels
Six multiple-choice questions about food energy, respiration, and comparing fuels. One group spokesperson per question.
⏱ 7 min 👥 Group
🏆
Grand Energy Quiz
All Topics
Team A vs Team B across all four topic areas. Ten questions, a live scoreboard, and a winner at the end.
⏱ 10 min 👥 Teams
