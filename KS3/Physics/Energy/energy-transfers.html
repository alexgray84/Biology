Step 3 · The big idea

The Law of Conservation of Energy

Energy is everywhere! But it follows one very strict rule.

The principle of the conservation of energy states that energy is never destroyed or used up. It is only transferred (changed) into another form.

When energy is transferred to make an appliance work, not all of it is useful. The energy that spreads out into the surroundings and cannot be used is called dissipation (wasted energy).
