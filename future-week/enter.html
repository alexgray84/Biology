the competition

How to enter

You enter your own work. Do it in form time, in a lesson, at home or in your own time. It is your job to send it in.

    1choose an entry route

    Eight activities count as competition entries. You may enter more than one, but only once for each.

      2do the work

      Follow the steps on the activity page. Work on paper. When you finish, tick your work against the activity’s three-point checklist. Fix anything that is missing before you send it.

      3send it in

      Use the entry form on your own device. If you can’t, hand in the paper. Both count the same.

      Way A Online, with the entry form

      1. Take a clear photo of your work with your own device. Lay it flat, in good light, with all of it in the picture. For a prototype, take one photo of it and one of its label.
      2. Open the entry form and sign in with your school account. If it says you need permission, you are in a personal Google account: switch to your school one.
      3. Fill in your name, year group, tutor group, the activity and where you did it. For a team, list every team member.
      4. Upload your photo. For Future Stories you can type or paste your story.
      5. Tick the boxes to say it is your own work, then press Submit. You will see “Thank you. Your entry is in.” If you don’t see that, it has not been sent.

      Way B If you can’t use the form: on paper, with an entry slip

      1. Fill in an entry slip. Print one, or copy it onto paper by hand: name, year, tutor group, team members, activity, where you did it, what you used AI for, and your signature.
      2. Fix the slip to the back of your work with a paper clip or tape.

      Print entry slips

      4know the rules

      • Your own work. You may get ideas from anyone. The work you send must be made by you, or by your team.
      • Using AI. Do your own thinking and make your own first attempt before you open Gemini or any other AI tool. After that you may use AI to check facts or to ask for feedback. Do not send in words or pictures that an AI made. If you used AI, say how on your entry.
      • Team entries. One person sends the entry and lists everyone in the team. Do not send it twice. A team with mixed year groups is judged in the age band of its oldest member.
      • One entry for each route. Enter as many routes as you like, once each.

      how it is judged

      On the activity’s own three-point checklist, plus one question for everyone: does it answer the question it started from?

      the awards

      11

      One for each of the five tracks in two age bands, Years 7 to 8 and Years 9 to 11, plus one Future Stories award.

      before you send

      Is your name on it? Is the photo clear? Did you tick all three checklist points? Did you pick the right activity?
