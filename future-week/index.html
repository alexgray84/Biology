how long have you got?

Ten minutes or a whole lesson. Every activity is written so you can run it yourselves, with no teacher. Tap a time and we’ll show what fits.

five tracks, fifteen questions

These are Dubai Future Week’s own questions. Pick the track you most want to answer.

pick one and start

Twenty activities and one bonus for Years 7 to 11, from ten minutes to a full lesson. Paper and a pen is all most of them need.

enter the competition

Eight activities double as competition entries. Every year group has a way in on every track.

the entry routes

    how to enter

    You send in your own work. Do the activity on paper and tick it against its three-point checklist. Then send a photo through the entry form from your own device. If you can’t, hand in the paper with an entry slip.

    How to enter, step by step

    how it is judged

    On the activity’s own three-point checklist, plus one question that is the same for everyone: does it answer the question it started from?

    the awards

    11

    One award for each of the five tracks in two age bands, Years 7 to 8 and Years 9 to 11, plus one Future Stories award.
