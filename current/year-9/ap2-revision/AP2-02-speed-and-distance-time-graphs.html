Speed and distance–time graphs

“Calculate speed and interpret/draw distance–time graphs.”

Big ideaSpeed = distance ÷ time, in m/s — convert km and minutes first. On a distance–time graph the height shows how far from the start, and the steepness shows the speed: flat = stopped, steeper = faster.

AReading a distance–time graph

Knowledge organiser · AP2 point 2 · part 2 of 3

BCalculating speed

speed = distance ÷ time  ·  v = d ÷ t  ·  m ÷ s → m/s
Worked: a racehorse runs 402 m in 20.6 s. speed = 402 ÷ 20.6 = 19.5 m/s.
Convert first: 2 km in 4 minutes. ✗ 2 ÷ 4 = 0.5 (units don’t match). ✓ 2000 m ÷ 240 s = 8.3 m/s.  1 km = 1000 m · 1 min = 60 s.
Whole journey: 30 m in 2 s, then 30 m in 4 s. Average speed = total distance ÷ total time = 60 ÷ 6 = 10 m/s — not the mean of 15 and 7.5. Stops count in the time.
Rearrange: distance = speed × time · time = distance ÷ speed.

CWhat the shape means

A curve that gets steeper = speeding up. Less steep = slowing down, but still moving away. The graph is not a picture of the road: a flat line is a stop, not a flat road.

DKey words and what not to confuse them with

SpeedHow far something travels each second. speed = distance ÷ time.≠ distance (how far altogether)
Average speedTotal distance ÷ total time for the whole journey, stops included.≠ the mean of the section speeds
Distance–time graphTime across, distance from the start up. Shows how far away the object is at each time.≠ a picture of the route or road
GradientThe steepness of the line: change in distance ÷ change in time = speed.≠ the height of the line
StationaryNot moving. A flat (horizontal) line: speed 0 m/s.≠ moving along flat ground
Constant speedEqual distances in equal times. A straight, sloping line.≠ speeding up

FMisconception busters

  • ✗ “The highest part of the graph is the fastest.”✓ Height is distance from the start. The steepest part is fastest.
  • ✗ “A flat line means a flat road.”✓ A flat line means stationary (0 m/s). The graph is not a picture of the route.
  • ✗ “Average speed = the mean of the section speeds.”✓ Use total distance ÷ total time, including the stop.
  • ✗ “2 km in 4 min = 2 ÷ 4 = 0.5 m/s.”✓ Convert first: 2000 m ÷ 240 s = 8.3 m/s.

GIn the UAE

Hatta mountain-bike trails: a rider’s GPS app draws a distance–time graph of the ride. The flat parts are rest stops, the steepest parts are the fastest sections — but the graph does not show the shape of the hills.

★ Going further (GCSE): velocity is speed in a stated direction. On a displacement–time graph, a line sloping down means moving back towards the start: a negative velocity.
