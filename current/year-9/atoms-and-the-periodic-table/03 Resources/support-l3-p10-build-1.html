The atoms have equal proton counts, p14, but different neutron counts, n14 and n16. They are different isotopes of silicon. Both have 14 electrons because they are neutral, but electron equality is not the isotope definition.

  • Use p14 to establish the same element.
  • Use n14 versus n16 to establish different isotopes.
  • Recognise electrons as unnecessary to this definition.
