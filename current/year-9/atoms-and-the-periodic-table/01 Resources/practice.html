Predict, test and explain a change

Predict in your book first. Test with the model or supplied particle cards. Explain each result.

  1. With eight protons and ten neutrons, predict the electrons needed for neutrality.
  2. Change only neutrons from ten to nine. Does charge change? Explain.
  3. Return to ten neutrons. Remove one electron. Predict the overall charge.
