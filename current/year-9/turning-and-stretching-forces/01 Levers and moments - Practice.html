moment = force × perpendicular distance → M = F × d

Part A · Calculate the moment everyone

Write M = F × d. Change every distance to metres. Give the unit N·m.

  1. A 10 N force acts 0.20 m from a pivot. It turns clockwise. Calculate the moment and state the direction.
    Answer. M = 10 × 0.20 = 2.0 N·m, clockwise.
  2. A 25 N force acts 0.40 m from a pivot. Calculate the moment.
    Answer. M = 25 × 0.40 = 10 N·m.
  3. A force of 8 N acts 60 cm from a pivot. Change the distance to metres, then find the moment.
    Answer. 60 cm = 0.60 m. M = 8 × 0.60 = 4.8 N·m.
  4. You push the end of a 0.30 m spanner with 40 N, at right angles. Calculate the moment.
    Answer. M = 40 × 0.30 = 12 N·m.
Build understanding. Put the force under “force” and the distance under “distance”, multiply, then add N·m.

Part B · Find the missing value everyone

Rearrange M = F × d. To find a force: F = M ÷ d. To find a distance: d = M ÷ F.

  1. A moment of 6.0 N·m is needed. The force acts 0.50 m from the pivot. Calculate the force.
    Answer. F = M ÷ d = 6.0 ÷ 0.50 = 12 N.
  2. A 15 N force gives a moment of 3.0 N·m. How far from the pivot does it act?
    Answer. d = M ÷ F = 3.0 ÷ 15 = 0.20 m.

Part C · Compare everyone

  1. Which has the larger moment: 12 N at 0.50 m, or 20 N at 0.25 m? Show both calculations.
    Answer. 12 × 0.50 = 6.0 N·m. 20 × 0.25 = 5.0 N·m. The 12 N force has the larger moment.

Part D · Go further when Part C is checked

  1. Explain why a long spanner makes a tight nut easier to turn. Use M = F × d in your answer.
    Answer. A longer spanner puts the force a larger distance d from the pivot (the nut). Because M = F × d, a larger d gives a larger moment for the same push, so the nut turns more easily.
  2. A door handle is placed far from the hinges. What changes for someone with less grip strength if the handle is moved close to the hinges? Use moments.
    Answer. Close to the hinges the distance d is small, so the moment is small. They would need a much larger force to open the door. Keeping the handle far from the hinges keeps the moment large, so a smaller force works.
