Virus role cards

Cut out the six cards. Each person in the group of six takes one card and acts out their stage in order. Everyone says what the host cell is doing.

Part A · Role cards cut out

1
Virus
You are a virus particle. Hold up your genetic material and your protein coat.
2
Attaches
Attach to the host cell. Say: "I stick to the surface of a host cell."
3
Genetic material enters
Pass your genetic material into the host cell. Your coat stays outside.
4
Host cell machinery
You are the host cell machinery. Make the new viral parts using the genetic material.
5
New viruses assemble
Put the viral parts together into new virus particles.
6
New viruses leave
Leave the host cell and move towards new cells to infect. Say: "The host cell is harmed."

Part B · After the role play everyone

  1. Which role is the host cell machinery? Why can a virus not reproduce without it?
    AnswerThe virus has no machinery of its own, so it needs the host cell to make new viral parts.
  2. Which stage harms the host cell?
    AnswerStage 6 when new viruses leave, and stage 4 when the cell is used to make viral parts.
