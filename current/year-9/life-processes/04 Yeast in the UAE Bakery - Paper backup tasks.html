AnswerIncrease = reading minus 8.0. Rate = increase ÷ 10. 15 °C: 2.5 cm, 0.25 cm/min; 25 °C: 4.5 cm, 0.45 cm/min; 35 °C: 7.5 cm, 0.75 cm/min; 40 °C: 9.0 cm, 0.90 cm/min; 50 °C: 6.5 cm, 0.65 cm/min; 60 °C: 0.5 cm, 0.05 cm/min. The rate peaks at 40 °C, is lower at 50 °C and is almost zero at 60 °C because the enzymes denature.
  1. Complete: Our results show that ____ °C produced the fastest respiration because …
    AnswerOur results show that 40 °C produced the fastest respiration because enzymes in yeast work fastest near this temperature, so more carbon dioxide was made (the balloon grew 9.0 cm compared with 2.5 cm at 15 °C).
  2. Calculate the rate of gas production at 35 °C: increase in circumference ÷ time.
    Answer7.5 ÷ 10 = 0.75 cm per minute
  3. Explain why the balloon grows so little at 60 °C.
    AnswerThe enzymes are denatured at high temperature: the active site changes shape, so respiration almost stops and little carbon dioxide is made.
  4. Challenge: why is a gas syringe a more accurate measure than balloon circumference?
    AnswerA syringe reads the volume of gas directly; a balloon stretches, so equal changes in circumference are not equal amounts of gas.
