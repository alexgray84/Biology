Part C · Misconception buster when Part B is checked

A student writes: “Respiration is breathing, because organisms breathe in oxygen to get energy.”

  1. Correct the statement in one sentence.
    AnswerRespiration is a chemical reaction in cells that releases energy from glucose. Breathing brings oxygen in so that cells can use it, but breathing is not respiration.
  2. Explain why the student is partly right.
    AnswerBreathing does supply the oxygen used in aerobic respiration, so the two processes are linked.
