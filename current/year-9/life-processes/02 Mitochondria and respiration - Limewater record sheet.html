Limewater practical: record sheet

Predict, observe, explain. Keep this safe from splashes.

NameClassDate
limewater + carbon dioxide → cloudy

Part A · Predict before you start

Limewater turns cloudy when carbon dioxide is bubbled through it. Before you start, predict what will happen when you breathe out gently through a straw into limewater.

I predict the limewater will ____________ because ____________________________.

AnswerIt will turn cloudy because breathed-out air contains carbon dioxide.

Part B · Safety check tick every box

  • Goggles on before I touch the limewater.
  • I will breathe out GENTLY.
  • I will never suck up through the straw.
  • I use a clean straw each time and put it in the bin after.
  • If limewater splashes in my eye I rinse with water and tell my teacher.

Part C · Record your results while you work

Breathed-out air

AnswerBefore: clear (colourless). After: cloudy or milky white. Number of breaths varies by pupil.

Part D · Explain after the practical

  1. What does the result show about breathed-out air?
    AnswerIt contains carbon dioxide.
  2. Where did that carbon dioxide come from? Use the word respiration.
    AnswerIt was made by respiration in cells, carried by the blood to the lungs and breathed out.
  3. Write the word equation for aerobic respiration.
    Answerglucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water

Part E · Go further if you finish

  1. Plan how you could use this apparatus to test how exercise changes breathing. Say what you change, measure and keep the same.
    AnswerChange: rest or after 1 minute of exercise. Measure: breaths or seconds until cloudy. Keep the same: person, volume and strength of limewater, how gently they blow.
  2. Why is judging a colour change by eye a weakness? How could you improve it?
    AnswerDifferent people may judge “cloudy” differently and the change is gradual. Use a light sensor or compare against a standard colour card.
