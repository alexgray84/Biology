Year 9 Biology: Respiration and Living Things

Mini quiz. 10 marks. Work alone for 8 minutes. Then mark in green pen with the mark scheme.

Part A · Questions 1 to 7 10 marks

  1. Tick ONE box. Which statement is correct? [1]

    Breathing is a chemical reaction that happens in cells.
    Respiration is the movement of air into and out of the lungs.
    Breathing brings in oxygen that cells use in respiration.
    Plants breathe but they do not respire.

    AnswerBreathing brings in oxygen that cells use in respiration. (1)
  2. Complete the word equation for aerobic respiration. [2]

    glucose + ______________ → ______________ + ______________

    Answeroxygen (1); carbon dioxide and water, either order (1).
  3. Balance the symbol equation by writing a number in each box. [2]

    C₆H₁₂O₆ + [  ] O₂ → [  ] CO₂ + [  ] H₂O

    Answer6 in the O₂ box (1); 6 in the CO₂ box and 6 in the H₂O box (1, both needed).
  4. Name the substance made when human muscle cells respire anaerobically. [1]
    AnswerLactic acid. (1)
  5. A sprinter breathes heavily for several minutes after a race. Explain why. [2]
    AnswerLactic acid built up during the race (anaerobic respiration) (1); extra oxygen is needed to break it down, which is repaying the oxygen debt (1).
  6. Yeast can respire without oxygen. Complete the word equation. [1]

    glucose → ______________ + ______________

    Answerethanol and carbon dioxide, either order, both needed (1).
  7. Viruses are not usually classed as living. Give one reason. [1]
    AnswerAny one: cannot reproduce on their own or need a host cell; not made of cells; do not respire or obtain nutrition on their own. (1)

Part B · Challenge unmarked

Challenge (not marked). A baker finds that dough rises faster in a warm kitchen than in a cold one. Explain why, using the word enzyme.

AnswerYeast respire to release carbon dioxide. Warmth increases enzyme activity, so respiration is faster and more carbon dioxide forms, but a very hot place would denature the enzymes.
