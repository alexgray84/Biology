Part 1 · Living Things Photo Hunt option 1

Find or photograph three organisms in your local environment. For each, give evidence for as many of the seven life processes (MRS GREN) as you can.

OrganismEvidence for each life process you can show
AnswerExample: date palm. Movement: only slow movement counts, such as fronds angling towards the light; being blown by the wind does not count. Growth: new fronds and a taller trunk. Reproduction: fruit and seeds. Nutrition: leaves make food. Sensitivity: new growth bends towards light. Respiration and excretion cannot be seen but happen in all cells.

Part 2 · Is it alive? challenge option 2

Explain which life processes each example shows, and which it does not.

ExampleShowsDoes not show
A plant
A cat
A seed
Fire
A robot
A crystal
AnswerPlant and cat: all seven. Seed: alive but dormant, shows few processes until it germinates. Fire: movement and uses fuel, but no cells, respiration, sensitivity or reproduction. Robot: movement and sensitivity (sensors), but no growth, respiration or reproduction. Crystal: grows, but does not respire, reproduce, excrete or feed.
