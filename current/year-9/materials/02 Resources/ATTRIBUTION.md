# Sources and original models

Models, diagrams and animation are original procedural teaching representations; no source images were copied. Qualitative, not calibrated simulations or experimental evidence.

- [Penn State · polymers](https://courses.ems.psu.edu/matse81/book/export/html/2209): Separate-chain thermoplastics and cross-linked thermosets; heating behaviour.
- [Composites Knowledge Network](https://compositeskn.org/KPC/A105): Thermoset transitions and chemical breakdown limits.
- [Australian Government · insulation](https://www.energy.gov.au/households/insulation-and-draught-proofing): Trapped relatively still air resists heat transfer.
