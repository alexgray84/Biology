L · Squeeze and pull words

Arrows in = pushed together = compression.
Arrows out = pulled apart = tension.
Draw one block with arrows in and write its word.
Open your new recheck

Go back only if you must. Answer on your whiteboard.
A tall post has one arrow pressing down on its top and one pressing up on its base. Name the load.

Check your recheck

Compression, because both arrows push towards the middle of the post.

B · Why both are better

Finish: “Concrete alone ___ when pulled. Steel in the pulled region ___. Together the beam can ___.”
“Both are strong” explains nothing. Name the two different loads.
Open your new recheck

Go back only if you must. Answer on your whiteboard.
Why would a beam pulled along its bottom be a poor job for plain concrete?

Check your recheck

Plain concrete is much weaker when pulled. With no steel to carry the pull, a crack can run through the beam and it can fail.
