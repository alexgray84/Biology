Depth cards

Year 9 Science · Materials

Depth · Change the supports

A concrete balcony is fixed to a wall at one end only. The diagram shows its top region pulled and its bottom region squeezed. Where should the main steel go? How is this different from our beam?

Depth · Find the counterexample

Claim: “Adding steel always makes concrete better.” Facts: steel is much denser than concrete, and steel can rust if water reaches it. Give a situation that challenges the claim.

Depth · Must bend easily

A part must now bend easily, like a rubber hose. An engineer says, “Reinforced concrete is a composite, so it will be suitable.” Evaluate the claim.

Depth · Push a rope

You can pull a rope tight. Try to imagine pushing one end to move the other end. What does this tell you about the word ‘strong’?

Depth · Is steel always needed?

A builder leaves the steel out of a pavement slab resting flat on firm ground. Is this always a poor choice? What would you want to know?

Depth · A different composite

Plywood is layers of wood glued with the grain at right angles. Someone says it must be better than a plank in every way. What evidence would test this?
