A self-heating lunch container

The cartridge reacts and the soup warms. Use the cutaway to trace the energy from its source to the soup.

Classify the reaction, identify the higher chemical energy level and explain how the sleeve helps. (4)

Model answer & mark scheme

The reaction is exothermic. Energy transfers from the reacting chemicals through the metal wall into the soup. The reactants have more chemical energy than the products. Foam reduces unwanted energy transfer to the room.

Mark points
  • Exothermic
