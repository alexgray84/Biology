Do now · Prior learning · energy and materials · 0–4 min

What does the thermometer tell us?

A burning fuel heats the pan of water. Name the chemical change. Draw an energy arrow from the reaction to what warms up.

Why would a foam cup slow the cooling of warm water?

Model answer & mark scheme

Burning is combustion (oxidation). Energy transfers from the reaction to the pan and water. Foam is a thermal insulator and reduces transfer to the room.

Success criteria
  • Name combustion or oxidation
  • Give the source and destination
  • Link insulation to slower transfer

Input · LO1–2 · worked example · 10–15 min

Follow the energy

The thermometer measures the temperature of its surroundings. Follow the arrow from the point of view of the reacting chemicals.

Worked example
  1. Iron reacts with oxygen. Energy transfers from the reacting chemicals to the pack and the hand.
  2. The surroundings gain energy and warm up. This reaction is exothermic.
  3. Reverse the arrow: an endothermic change takes in energy from its surroundings. They cool if no external heating offsets the change.

Where did the energy come from?

Model answer & mark scheme

Model answer & mark scheme
