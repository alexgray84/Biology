Depth cards

Year 9 Science · Materials

Depth · The washing stops

A school’s event moves to a site with no way of washing cups. Does a recommendation based on reuse change? Justify your answer.

Depth · Find the counterexample

Claim: “Reusable is always better than single-use.” Describe a situation where this would not follow from the evidence.

Depth · Swap the feedstock

A polymer is made from plants instead of crude oil. It is chemically identical to the oil-based version. Is it more biodegradable? Explain.

Depth · Change the energy

A brick works replaces its fuel-burning kiln with one powered by renewable electricity. Which link in the chain changes? Which link does not change?
