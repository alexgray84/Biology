Question: How does the release height of a ramp affect the average speed of a toy car over a fixed distance?

Before you start

Distance travelled: m    Keep the same car, ramp, surface, measured distance, start line and timing endpoint each time.

  1. Use only the three release heights marked by your teacher.
  2. Release the car — do not push it. Start timing when the car crosses the start line at the foot of the ramp; stop when the front reaches the finish line.
  3. Keep the route clear. Do not reach into a moving car’s path.
  4. Record every result to the nearest 0.1 s, including an unusual one.
