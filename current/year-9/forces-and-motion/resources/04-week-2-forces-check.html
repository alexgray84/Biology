1 · Resultant force 2 marks

A go-kart is driven along a flat track. The driving force is 260 N. Air resistance and friction together are 180 N.

a) Calculate the resultant force and state its direction.   b) Describe what happens to the kart's speed.

2 · Force or energy? 2 marks

Here are two statements about a cyclist going downhill.

A: "Gravity pulls the bike down the slope, so it speeds up."   B: "Energy moves from the gravitational store to the kinetic store, so the bike speeds up."

a) Which statement is about a force and which is about energy?   b) Correct this sentence: "The kinetic energy pushes the bike down the hill."

4 · Will it balance? 3 marks

A metre rule rests on a pivot at its centre. A 6 N weight hangs 0.4 m to the left of the pivot. A 4 N weight hangs 0.5 m to the right.

a) Calculate the moment on each side.   b) Predict whether the rule balances. If not, say which side goes down.   c) Explain your prediction using the words force and distance from the pivot.
