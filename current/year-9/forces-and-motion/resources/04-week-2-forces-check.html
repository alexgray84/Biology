1. Resultant force · 2 marks

A kart moves forwards along a flat track. Driving force is 260 N forwards; air resistance and friction total 180 N backwards.

a) Calculate the resultant force and state its direction.
b) Describe what happens to the kart’s speed.

2. Force or energy? · 2 marks

A: “Gravity pulls the bike down the slope, so it speeds up.”
B: “Energy transfers from the gravitational store to the kinetic store as the bike speeds up.”

a) Which statement describes a force, and which describes energy?
b) Correct: “Kinetic energy pushes the bike down the hill.”

3. Speed · 3 marks

A toy car travels 1.5 m in each trial. Its times are 2.4 s, 2.6 s and 2.5 s.

a) Calculate the mean time.
b) Calculate the average speed, with its unit.
c) A lower release point gives a time of 3.0 s over the same distance. Without calculating, say whether the speed is higher or lower and why.

4. Will it balance? · 3 marks

A uniform metre rule is pivoted at its centre. A 6 N weight hangs 0.4 m left of the pivot; a 4 N weight hangs 0.5 m right.

a) Calculate each moment.
b) Predict whether it balances; if not, say which side goes down.
c) Explain using force and distance from the pivot.
