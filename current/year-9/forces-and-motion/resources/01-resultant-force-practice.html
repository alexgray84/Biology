Part B · Calculate the resultant everyone

  1. A skydiver has a weight of 700 N. The air resistance on her is 500 N. Calculate the resultant force and give its direction. Explain what happens to her speed.
  2. She opens her parachute. The air resistance jumps to 2000 N. Calculate the new resultant force and explain how her speed changes.
  3. A cyclist pedals along a flat road with a driving force of 70 N. Friction is 5 N and air resistance is 55 N. Draw the horizontal forces, calculate the resultant and describe the motion.
  4. A delivery van has a driving force of 1200 N. Air resistance and friction together are 900 N. State the resultant and describe the motion. Then say what must change for the van to travel at a steady speed.
  5. In a tug of war, one team pulls with 350 N and the other with 350 N. State the resultant. Explain why the rope does not move even though two large forces act on it.
Build understanding: use this frame. "The resultant is __ N (larger force minus smaller force) acting __. Because the resultant is __, the object will __."

Part C · Go further when Part B is checked

  1. A lorry and a car brake with the same braking force. The lorry takes longer to stop. Use the idea of resultant force to explain why a heavier vehicle needs a longer braking distance, and say why road signs give lorries lower speed limits.
  2. A rowing boat is moving at a steady speed. The rowers pull harder for ten strokes, then go back to their normal pull. Describe the forces and the motion during each stage, including what happens to water resistance as the boat gets faster.

Part D · Check an AI claim everyone, last five minutes

A pupil asked a chatbot to explain steady speed. Here is its answer.

"A car moving at a steady speed has no forces acting on it. The forces are balanced, so they cancel each other out and disappear. If the driver wants to speed up, the engine force just needs to be larger than zero."
  1. Underline every part of the claim that the force model disproves.
  2. Rewrite the claim so that it is correct. Use the words balanced, resultant and resistance.
  3. State one thing the chatbot got right.
  4. Write one sentence: what evidence would make the chatbot change its answer?
