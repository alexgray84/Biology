    Model a structure–function link

    Follow the explanation. Annotate the feature that does the job.

    • A root hair cell has a long extension. This increases its surface area for absorption of water and mineral ions.
    • It is not a photosynthetic leaf cell; do not add chloroplasts just because it is a plant cell.

    Complete a new caption

    Work in pairs, then record your own caption.

    • A ciliated epithelial cell lines an airway. Its cilia beat to move mucus containing trapped particles.
    • Complete: “The cell has ___, which ___, helping ___.”

    Which sentence explains?

    Choose the best explanation on your whiteboard.

      Which is a complete explanation?

      1. The cell has a long extension.
      2. The extension makes the cell photosynthesise.
      3. The extension increases surface area for absorption.
