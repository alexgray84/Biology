Plan a sports-session explanation

Read the brief. Identify the science the audience needs.

  • A school in the UAE wants a small display explaining the body’s cooling responses.
  • It must explain mechanisms. It must not claim that sweating makes strenuous activity safe in all conditions.

Two main cooling routes

Follow both routes. Label the heat leaving the body in your record.

  • The hypothalamus helps coordinate temperature responses using information about core and skin temperature.
  • Sweat evaporates from skin. Evaporation transfers energy away and cools the skin.
  • Skin arterioles widen (vasodilation). More blood reaches the skin surface, allowing more heat transfer to cooler surroundings.

Use a small effect carefully

Compare the two descriptions. Record which is more accurate and why.

  • A: Flattening hairs removes most heat from a human body.
  • B: Flattening hairs reduces the trapped insulating air layer, but this effect is small in humans.
  • In cold conditions, vasoconstriction reduces skin blood flow; shivering produces more heat through muscle activity.
