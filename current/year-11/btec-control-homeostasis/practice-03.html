What homeostasis means

Read the model. Draw a box around the controlled variable.

  • Homeostasis keeps internal conditions relatively stable, within suitable ranges.
  • Temperature and blood glucose can vary around a normal level; they are not fixed exactly.
  • Receptors detect change. A coordination system activates effectors. Their response opposes the change.

Think through one loop

Follow the model, then record when the response should reduce.

  • Model incubator: temperature above range → sensor detects rise → controller turns heater down → temperature falls.
  • As temperature approaches the chosen range, the corrective response reduces.
  • The incubator is an analogy. The body uses cells, nervous signals and hormones.

Why is it called negative feedback?

Choose one answer. Give a reason on your whiteboard.

    What makes feedback negative?

    1. The response opposes the original deviation.
    2. It always lowers the controlled variable.
    3. It damages the organism.
