A message carried by blood

Follow the arrows. Record the route in four short stages.

  • An endocrine gland secretes a hormone into the blood.
  • Blood carries the hormone around the body.
  • Target cells with the correct receptors respond. Other cells may encounter it without responding.

Use named examples

Read each example. Match the gland to its hormone in your record.

  • Adrenal glands above the kidneys release adrenaline. It helps prepare the body for action, including a faster heartbeat.
  • The pancreas releases insulin when blood glucose is high. It helps lower blood glucose.
  • A hormone is a chemical message, not a nerve impulse.

    Why does one cell respond?

    Choose one answer. Give a reason on your whiteboard.

      A hormone passes many cells but only some respond. Why?

      1. Blood carries it only to those cells.
      2. All cells respond in exactly the same way.
      3. Only some cells have the appropriate receptors.
