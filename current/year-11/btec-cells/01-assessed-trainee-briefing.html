Assessed task: Brief a laboratory trainee

Date: today’s date

Draw a large box in your exercise book. Highlight its border. Leave the inside clear for your answers.

Write the title and date at the top. Put all your answers and working inside this box.

Before you begin

  1. Set up your highlighted box as shown.
  2. Read “What I will mark”.
  3. Open the task and wait for the teacher’s start instruction.

Work alone. Close other pages and cover earlier notes. Use this task page only.

Number your answers clearly. Keep your first answers unchanged for teacher marking.

Your teacher will mark the work in your book. You do not need a printed sheet. This is classroom practice, not a Pearson Set Assignment.

Your laboratory brief

An airway cell has moving cilia. A root hair cell has a long extension into soil.

Prepare a short briefing for a new laboratory trainee.

Write independently

  1. Explain how each named feature supports the cell’s function.
  2. Write a short briefing that a new trainee could understand.
  3. State why one feature would not suit the other cell.
  4. Add one limitation: what cannot be concluded from the description alone?

Write in your highlighted book box. Keep the original answers unchanged; add corrections below the box after teacher feedback.

Three things your teacher will assess

K

Knowledge

Name the cell structures and biological processes accurately.

A

Application

Explain how structure supports function.

D

Disciplinary skill

Explain clearly and recognise what the evidence cannot establish.
