Fact-check an AI explanation

Prepared for this lesson. No live AI tool or personal information is needed.

1 · Think first

Before you look: write one sentence in your book on how blood flow helps gas exchange.

2 · The AI-style explanation

“Oxygen diffuses from the blood into the alveolus. Thin walls speed up exchange because the distance is short. Slower blood flow speeds up exchange because there is more time. A moist lining lets gases dissolve.”

Confident wording is not evidence.

3 · Check each claim

Copy the grid. For each claim write accept, reject or can’t check with this model, and your evidence.

Claim 1: show O₂ only. Claim 2: thin vs thick wall. Claim 3: normal vs slow flow. Claim 4: can the model show moisture? If not, where would you check?

4 · Decide and reflect

Rewrite the paragraph so every claim is right, in your own words. Which mistake sounded most convincing, and why?

Your teacher will check your grid and rewrite.
