Repair the ray model

Diagnose errors, test a prepared AI claim and apply the model. Think first; then use evidence to improve your answer.

Three common errors

  1. A pupil measures the angle of reflection from the mirror.
  2. A pupil draws a plane-mirror image on the mirror surface.
  3. A pupil draws a ray bending away from the normal as it enters acrylic from air.

For each error

Name the error, state the correct rule and sketch the repaired diagram.

Non-negotiable rules

  • Reflection angles are measured from the normal.
  • For a plane mirror, object distance = image distance.
  • The virtual image sits behind the mirror.
  • Air → acrylic: ray bends towards the normal.
  • Rectangular block traced from air: the ray bends away from the normal as it exits acrylic.
  • Along the normal: no change of direction.

Human first

Draw a reflected ray when the incident ray meets the mirror at 40° to the normal. Label i and r. Commit before reading the prepared response.

Your evidence

Write the law or measurement that proves your ray is correct.

Prepared AI response

AI assistant

“The incident ray is 40° from the normal, so it is 50° from the mirror. The reflected ray should therefore be drawn 50° from the normal because reflection swaps the two angles.”

  1. Underline the exact false claim.
  2. Use the reflection law to disprove it.
  3. Rewrite the response accurately in no more than two sentences.
  4. Name one part the response got right.

UAE solar-mirror challenge

A solar research site uses mirrors to redirect sunlight towards a receiver. A ray reaches one mirror at 30° to the normal.

  1. Draw and label the reflected ray.
  2. State the angle of reflection.
  3. Rotate the mirror clockwise by 10°. Redraw the normal and predict how the reflected direction changes.
  4. Explain why accurate mirror alignment matters for research and energy technology.

Final quality check

  • The mirror and normal are at 90°.
  • Angle arcs touch the normal.
  • i = r.
  • The explanation links mirror orientation, normal direction and reflected path.
  • Your AI correction is based on scientific evidence, not whether the wording sounded confident.
