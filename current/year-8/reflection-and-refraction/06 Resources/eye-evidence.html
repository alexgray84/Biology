Explain how the eye works

Original classroom questions · 14 marks

Work independently. Close the model and your notes. Draw and write in your book.

A new view

The front of this eye is on the left. Copy the outline and label the six core structures. Ciliary muscle and suspensory ligaments are optional extension labels.

1. Add arrows to show light entering and reaching the retina. Use a different line style for information travelling onwards.

2. Explain the jobs of the cornea, lens, retina and optic nerve.

3. A person moves into brighter daylight. Explain what happens to the pupil and what controls that change.

Finish by checking arrow directions and the difference between a structure and an opening.
