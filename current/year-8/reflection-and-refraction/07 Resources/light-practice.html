Year 8 science · Work in your exercise book

Choose the rule, then solve

For each question, first write the rule you need. Then draw or explain. Use a ruler for rays. Try each item before opening support in the lesson.

1. A ray makes 20° with the mirror surface. Draw a normal and calculate its angle of reflection.

2. A ray enters water from air along the normal. Draw its direction in the water. Explain your choice.

3. A pupil claims that a mirror image lies on the mirror surface. An object is 6 cm in front of the mirror. Correct the claim.

4. Draw the route from a torch to a wall and then into an eye.

5. Compare the jobs of the iris and lens. Explain why a smaller pupil does not mean a smaller lens.

6. A ray passes obliquely from glass into air. Predict its direction relative to the normal. Assume it passes out of the glass.
