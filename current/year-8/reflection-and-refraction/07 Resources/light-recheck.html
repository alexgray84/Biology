Original classroom questions · 10 marks

Work independently in your book. Close notes, support and the model. Answer all six questions. This is practice, not Thursday’s assessment.

1. Draw how a torch makes a wall visible to a person.

2. A ray meets a plane mirror at 25° to the normal. State its angle of reflection.

3. An object is 8 cm in front of a plane mirror. Locate its image and state one property.

4. A ray passes obliquely out of glass into air. Draw the normal and the refracted direction. Assume it passes out.

5. Name the eye structure that detects light and state what travels along the optic nerve.

6. A person enters a dim room. Explain the change in pupil size and name the controlling structure.
