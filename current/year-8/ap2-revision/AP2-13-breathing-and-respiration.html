Knowledge organiser · AP2 point 13 · Biology

Breathing and respiration

“Explain the difference between breathing and respiration.”

Big ideaBreathing (ventilation) moves air in and out of the lungs, using muscles. Gas exchange moves oxygen and carbon dioxide between the alveoli and the blood by diffusion. Respiration is a chemical reaction in every living cell that releases usable energy from glucose.

AThe breathing system

Air route: nose/mouth → trachea → bronchus → bronchioles → alveoli. Breathing in: diaphragm and intercostals contract → chest volume ↑ → pressure inside ↓ → air in.

BBreathing vs respiration

BreathingRespiration
Whatmoving air in and out of the lungsa chemical reaction that releases usable energy
Wherelungs and chestevery living cell, in the mitochondria
Howmuscles change chest volume and pressureglucose reacts with oxygen
Whoanimals with lungsall living things, plants too

glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water (energy released)

The link: breathing brings in the oxygen that cells use in respiration, and removes the carbon dioxide it makes.

DKey words and what not to confuse them with

Breathing (ventilation)Moving air into and out of the lungs.≠ respiration
RespirationA chemical reaction in all living cells (in the mitochondria) that releases usable energy from glucose.≠ breathing
Gas exchangeO₂ and CO₂ moving between the alveoli and the blood by diffusion.≠ breathing in and out
AlveoliTiny air sacs with thin walls beside capillaries: the gas-exchange surface.≠ bronchioles (airways)
DiaphragmA sheet of muscle below the lungs that changes the chest volume.≠ an air passage
Intercostal musclesMuscles between the ribs that move the ribs up and out.≠ the ribs themselves

FMisconception busters

  • ✗ “Respiration is breathing.”✓ Breathing moves air. Respiration is a reaction in cells that releases energy.
  • ✗ “Respiration only happens in the lungs.”✓ It happens in every living cell, including muscle, brain and plant cells.
  • ✗ “We breathe out all the oxygen we breathe in as carbon dioxide.”✓ Breathed-out air still has about 16% oxygen; nitrogen stays about 78%.
  • ✗ “The diaphragm pushes air out by contracting.”✓ In quiet breathing out it relaxes and domes up; volume falls, pressure rises.

GIn the UAE

Pearl divers of the Gulf held their breath for over a minute on each dive. Breathing stopped, but respiration did not: their cells kept using the oxygen already in their lungs and blood, and carbon dioxide built up until they surfaced.

★ Going further: the energy released by respiration is passed to a molecule called ATP, which carries it to wherever the cell needs it, e.g. muscle contraction.

6Exam technique for this point

  • Three different processesBreathing moves air. Gas exchange moves gases by diffusion. Respiration is a reaction in cells. Keep them in separate sentences.
  • Energy is released, not madeWrite “respiration releases usable energy”, never “makes energy”.
  • Say whereBreathing: the lungs and chest. Gas exchange: the alveoli. Respiration: every living cell.
  • Percentage points21% → 16% is a fall of 5 percentage points. Show the subtraction.
  • Breathing chain in orderMuscles contract → volume up → pressure inside down → air in. One link per step.
