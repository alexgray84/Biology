Knowledge organiser · AP2 point 14 · Biology

Diet, digestion and energy in food

“Describe a balanced diet, energy requirements and the main organs/functions of the digestive system; interpret the energy-in-food practical.”

Big ideaA balanced diet has a variety of foods in the right proportions, giving every nutrient. How much energy you need depends on your age, size and activity. Digestion breaks big, insoluble food into small, soluble molecules that the small intestine absorbs into the blood.

AA balanced diet

NutrientJobFound in
Carbohydratesmain source of energyrice, bread, dates
Lipids (fats)stored energy; insulationolive oil, butter
Proteingrowth and repairfish, eggs, lentils
Vitaminshealth, e.g. vitamin Cfruit, vegetables
Mineralscalcium: bones; iron: bloodmilk, spinach
Fibrekeeps food moving in the gutwholegrains
Waterneeded by every cellwater, fruit

Energy needs (illustrative, per day): 13-year-old 9 000 kJ, or 11 500 kJ if sporty; adult builder 14 000 kJ. Imbalance: energy in > used → stored as fat → obesity; energy in < used → body mass falls (starvation). Too little of one nutrient → deficiency disease: no vitamin C → scurvy; too little vitamin D or calcium → rickets. Labels give energy in kJ and kcal (1 kcal ≈ 4.2 kJ).

BThe digestive system

Food’s route: mouth → oesophagus → stomach → small intestine → large intestine → rectum → anus. Mechanical digestion: teeth, stomach churning. Chemical: enzymes — amylase: starch → sugars; protease: protein → amino acids; lipase: fats → fatty acids + glycerol. Bile breaks fat into droplets. Gut bacteria in the large intestine help break down fibre and make some vitamins.

CThe energy-in-food practical

Change (IV)type of food
Measure (DV)temperature rise of the water / °C
Controlvolume of water · mass of food · distance from tube
Improvedraught shield / insulate the tube · relight until it won’t burn · stir, read the highest temperature

energy (J) = mass of water (g) × 4.2 × temperature rise (°C)

Example: 20 g water rises 25 °C: 20 × 4.2 × 25 = 2100 J. Food was 0.5 g, so 2100 ÷ 0.5 = 4200 J/g. Safety: goggles, no nuts (allergies), tie hair back.

DKey words and what not to confuse them with

Balanced dietA variety of foods in the right proportions to stay healthy.≠ eating less, or only “healthy” foods
NutrientA substance in food the body needs for energy, growth and health.≠ a food
DigestionBreaking food into small, soluble molecules the body can absorb.≠ absorption
AbsorptionSmall food molecules passing into the blood (in the small intestine).≠ digestion
EnzymeA protein that speeds up the breakdown of food molecules.≠ acid
Energy requirementThe energy a person needs from food each day, in kJ. Depends on age, size and activity.≠ the energy in one meal

FMisconception busters

  • ✗ “A balanced diet means eating fewer calories.”✓ It means the right proportions of all nutrients, with energy that matches what you use.
  • ✗ “Everyone needs the same amount of energy.”✓ Needs depend on age, size, sex and activity; growing teenagers need a lot.
  • ✗ “Food is absorbed in the stomach.”✓ Most absorption into the blood happens in the small intestine.
  • ✗ “The practical measures all the energy in the food.”✓ Lots of energy is lost heating the air and the tube, so the result is too low.

GIn the UAE

Breaking the fast: in Ramadan many families open the fast with dates and water. Dates are rich in carbohydrate (sugar), a quick energy source, and water replaces what was lost in the heat. A balanced iftar then adds protein, vegetables and fibre.

★ Going further: the 4.2 in the equation is the specific heat capacity of water — it takes 4.2 J to warm 1 g of water by 1 °C. For oil it is only about 2 J/g °C, so the same energy warms 1 g of oil about twice as much.

6Exam technique for this point

  • Use the temperature riseEnergy uses the rise (end − start), never the end temperature on its own.
  • Equation, substitute, answer, unitEnergy = 20 × 4.2 × 30 = 2520 J. Then ÷ mass of food for J/g.
  • Reason → matching improvement“Energy heated the air → use a draught shield.” Each improvement must fix the reason you gave.
  • Organs: name and job“The small intestine absorbs small food molecules into the blood” scores; “the intestine digests food” is too vague.
