Knowledge organiser · AP2 point 11 · Biology

Plant, animal and bacterial cells

“Compare plant, animal and bacterial cells and identify their main structures.”

Big ideaAll living things are made of cells. Every cell has a cell membrane and cytoplasm. Plant cells add a cell wall, a large permanent vacuole and (in some cells) chloroplasts. Bacteria have no nucleus: their DNA is a loop in the cytoplasm.

AAnimal and plant cells

Plant extras: cell wall (outside the membrane) · large permanent vacuole · chloroplasts in cells that get light. Both have mitochondria.

Knowledge organiser · AP2 point 11 · part 2 of 3

BA bacterial cell

No nucleus: the DNA is one loop in the cytoplasm, plus plasmids. No mitochondria, no chloroplasts. The wall is not cellulose. Some bacteria have a flagellum to move.

DKey words and what not to confuse them with

Cell membraneControls what enters and leaves the cell. In every cell.≠ cell wall
Cell wallSupports and strengthens the cell. It is outside the membrane.≠ cell membrane
NucleusContains genetic information (DNA) and controls the cell’s activities.≠ the whole cell
MitochondriaWhere aerobic respiration releases usable energy. Plant and animal cells.≠ chloroplasts
ChloroplastsContain chlorophyll, which absorbs light for photosynthesis. Only some plant cells.≠ mitochondria
PlasmidA small extra ring of DNA found in bacteria.≠ the nucleus

FMisconception busters

  • ✗ “Plant cells have a cell wall instead of a membrane.”✓ Plant cells have both. The wall is outside the membrane.
  • ✗ “Every plant cell has chloroplasts.”✓ Only cells that get light. A root cell has none, but it is still a plant cell.
  • ✗ “Only animal cells have mitochondria.”✓ Plant cells respire too, so they have mitochondria as well.
  • ✗ “Bacteria have no DNA because they have no nucleus.”✓ They have DNA — a loop free in the cytoplasm, plus plasmids.

GIn the UAE

Laban and yoghurt: the bacteria that turn milk into yoghurt in a UAE dairy are single cells. They have a cell wall, a membrane, cytoplasm and a DNA loop — but no nucleus. The date palm leaf cells that make the dates’ sugar have chloroplasts; its root cells do not.

★ Going further: plant and animal cells are eukaryotic (DNA in a nucleus); bacteria are prokaryotic (no nucleus) and much smaller.

They both have a membrane. The bacteria has no organs.

Similarity 1 ✓. “No organs” names no structure, so no difference mark.

6Exam technique for this point

  • Name the exact structure“Cell wall”, not “wall”; “cell membrane”, not “skin”. Chlorophyll is a chemical — the structure is a chloroplast.
  • Compare = both sidesEach difference needs both cells: “The plant cell has…, but the animal cell does not.”
  • Count the points“Two similarities and two differences” = four separate points. Number them.
  • Bacteria: say where the DNA isNever just “no nucleus”. Add: “its DNA is a single loop in the cytoplasm”.
  • Judge from the evidenceDecide the cell type from what it has (wall, vacuole, nucleus), not just from what is missing.
