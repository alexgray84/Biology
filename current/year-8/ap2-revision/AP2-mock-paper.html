Section A · Multiple choice tap one answer

1A pupil reads a book by the light of a lamp. Why can the pupil see the book? [1]

B. The book is non-luminous: it reflects (scatters) the lamp’s light, and that light enters the eye and is detected by the retina. Light never travels out of the eye (A, D), and a book makes no light of its own (C).

2Earth is closest to the Sun in early January. Why is January still a cool month in the UAE? [1]

A. Seasons are caused by the tilt of Earth’s axis, not its distance from the Sun. In January the northern hemisphere (with the UAE) is tilted away: the Sun is lower, so its energy is spread over a bigger area, and the days are shorter. B, C and D are not true.

Section A · Multiple choice tap one answer

3A pupil points a pinhole camera at a palm tree. How does the image on the screen look, and why? [1]

B. Light from the top of the tree travels in a straight line through the pinhole to the bottom of the screen, and light from the bottom reaches the top, so the image is inverted. Light does not bend at a pinhole (A); the screen does not flip it (C); a brain is not involved in a camera (D).

4A football match in Spain is shown live on TV in the UAE. Which use of satellites makes this possible? [1]

C. Communications satellites receive the TV signal and send it on around the curved Earth. Weather satellites image clouds (A); navigation satellites give your position (B); Earth-observation satellites photograph the ground (D).

Section A · Multiple choice tap one answer

5A watermelon from a market in Al Ain has a mass of 2.5 kg. What is its weight on Earth? (g = 10 N/kg) [1]

C. W = m × g = 2.5 kg × 10 N/kg = 25 N. A (÷ 10) and D (× 100) use the wrong operation; B confuses mass with weight.

6A ball rolls along a flat floor and slowly comes to a stop. Which distance–time graph shows this? [1]

B: the curve gets less and less steep (slowing down) until it is flat (stopped). A is a steady speed, C is speeding up, and D is impossible: total distance travelled never goes down.

Section A · Multiple choice tap one answer

7A racing camel runs 1.8 km in 3 minutes. What is its average speed in m/s? [1]

C. Convert first: 1.8 km = 1800 m and 3 min = 180 s. Speed = 1800 ÷ 180 = 10 m/s. A is km per minute (1.8 ÷ 3), B multiplies (1.8 × 3), D forgets to change minutes into seconds (1800 ÷ 3).

8Which structure is found in a ghaf tree leaf cell but not in a bacterial cell? [1]

D. Chloroplasts (for photosynthesis) are found in leaf cells but not in bacteria. Both the leaf cell and the bacterium have a cell wall (A), a cell membrane (B) and cytoplasm (C); the bacterium’s DNA is a loop in the cytoplasm, not in a nucleus.

Section A · Multiple choice tap one answer

9Which of these is breathing, not respiration? [1]

B. Breathing (ventilation) is the muscle movement that moves air in and out of the lungs. A, C and D all describe respiration: a chemical process inside living cells, including plant cells.

10The ribs surround the heart and lungs. Which function of the skeleton does this show? [1]

B. The rib cage protects the heart and lungs from damage. Support (A) holds the body up; blood cells are made in bone marrow (C); movement (D) needs muscles pulling on bones at joints.
