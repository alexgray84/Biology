Digestive-system depth cards

Open to all. Depth depends on your reasoning, not speed. Language or access support can stay.

A changed lining

Think silently for 20 seconds. Enzymes still break starch into small sugars, but there are fewer villi. Write a claim and reasons: what changes, and what can still happen? Then answer: why can digestion continue when absorption falls?

Model answer & criteria

Model response Digestion can still happen because the enzymes remain active. Absorption is slower/reduced because less surface area is available.

Success criteria Digestion and absorption separated. · Less surface area linked to reduced uptake. · Rejects “no digestion”.

Judge the tights model

Think silently for 20 seconds. Squeezing tights produces drips. Write a claim and reasons: does this prove enzyme digestion or diffusion? Say one useful mapping and one limit; improve the explanation. Probe: what evidence would distinguish chemical breakdown from liquid crossing holes?

Model answer & criteria

Model judgement It can represent food moving along a tube and material crossing a boundary. Drips caused by pressure through holes do not prove chemical breakdown or diffusion. Improve the explanation by naming the analogy and separating enzyme action from absorption.

Follow-up probe To distinguish chemical digestion, test for smaller molecular products; observing drips alone is insufficient.

Success criteria A defensible useful mapping. · Both limits stated. · A specific accurate improvement. · Probe: test for smaller molecular products, not drips alone.
