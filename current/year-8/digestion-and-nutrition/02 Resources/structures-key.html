1. Food route

mouth → oesophagus → stomach → small intestine → large intestine → rectum → anus.

A → D → F → J → I → K → L. Teeth are within the mouth, not an extra canal stop.

2. Structure and function

C: makes saliva containing amylase. H: supplies digestive enzymes into the small intestine. G: stores/releases bile; the liver makes it.

3. Villus explanation

A thin lining gives a short distance for molecules to cross, supporting rapid absorption. The crossing is absorption, not chemical digestion.

Criteria

Label endpoints land on the named structures. Route contains the seven canal stops in order and no accessory organs. Organ functions are accurately matched. The villus answer connects feature to effect, rather than naming a feature alone.
