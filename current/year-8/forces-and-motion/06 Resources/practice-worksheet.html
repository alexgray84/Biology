Distance–time graphs: practice worksheet

Year 8 science · illustrative data · answer on this sheet or in your book.

1. Reconstruct a journey

A model rover travels 10 m in 10 s, waits 10 s, then travels another 30 m in 10 s.

Write the cumulative coordinates, including (0,0). Explain which moving section is faster using the distance travelled in equal times.

2. Draw and explain independently · 10 marks

Optional support strip

First read a time, then its distance. For an explanation: “From … to …, distance stays …, so …”. Try a fresh interval without the sentence frame when ready. The default blank grid leaves scale selection to you.

Guided practice

(0,0), (10,10), (20,10), (30,40). Final moving section: 30 m in 10 s, versus 10 m in 10 s. Unmarked: use cumulative distance and unchanged distance during the stop.

Independent task: model graph

Stop: 10–20 s, lasting 10 s; total distance remains 10 m. Fastest: 20–30 s, covering 30 m in 10 s.

Original classroom mark scheme · 10 marks

  • Axes assigned correctly [1]; labels and units [1]; consistent scales [1].
  • All five points correct [2]; three or four correct [1]. Successive straight joins [1].
  • Stop interval and duration [1]; unchanged-distance explanation [1].
  • Fastest interval [1]; correct equal-time distance or same-scale slope comparison [1].
