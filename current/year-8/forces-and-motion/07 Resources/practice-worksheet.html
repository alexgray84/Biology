Calculating and comparing speed: practice worksheet

Year 8 science · illustrative data · answer on this sheet or in your book.

1. Practise the method

a. Average speed = distance ÷ time. Example: 20 m in 10 s → 2 m/s. Now calculate 24 m in 8 s.

b. A straight graph section joins (5 s,10 m) and (15 s,30 m). Complete: distance change = …; time change = …; speed = … .

c. For m/s, convert minutes to seconds first. Calculate 180 m in 2 minutes.

2. Calculate independently · 11 marks

Show your quantities, working and units. Keep your first attempt before reviewing.

A model car travels 42 m in 7 s. Calculate its average speed. [2]

A straight graph section joins (10 s,12 m) and (20 s,42 m). Calculate its speed. [3]

A rover travels 40 m in 20 s, stops for 10 s, then travels 40 m in 20 s. A report gives 2 m/s as the whole-journey average. Calculate the correct average and explain the error. [3]

A walking journey covers 120 m in 2 minutes. Calculate average speed in m/s. [3]

Optional support strip

For a section, subtract the two endpoint readings on each axis. For a whole journey, add every section time, including stops. Use minutes × 60 to find seconds. Then distance ÷ time. Remove the procedure prompt for a fresh attempt when ready; keep agreed access arrangements.

Guided practice · unmarked

a. 24/8 = 3 m/s. b. (30−10)/(15−5) = 20/10 = 2 m/s. c. 2 min = 120 s; 180/120 = 1.5 m/s.
