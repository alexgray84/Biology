An instrument on Earth and the Moon

Original classroom questions · 10 marks

Fictional UAE engineering brief · Supplied classroom values

A sealed instrument has a mass of 2 kg. A team moves it from Earth to the Moon without adding or removing any material. Work independently in your book.
