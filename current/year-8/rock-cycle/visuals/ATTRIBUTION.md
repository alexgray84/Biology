# Visual sources

- **Apollo 17 Earth photograph.** Image courtesy of the Earth Science and Remote Sensing Unit, NASA Johnson Space Center. Photograph AS17-148-22727, 7 December 1972. [NASA caption](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/the-blue-marble/) · [Image record and download](https://eol.jsc.nasa.gov/SearchPhotos/photo.pl?frame=22727&mission=AS17&roll=148) · [NASA media usage guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/)
- **Earth cutaways.** Original classroom illustrations. The crust is visibly exaggerated so pupils can identify its position. Colours mark the layers and do not represent observed interior colours. Labels follow the department’s Lesson 1 and the USGS description of Earth’s interior. [Department Lesson 1](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1hCzoFeyFO9LOFDoxdbzc-gkh9SMNI6oh/edit) · [USGS cutaway and layer descriptions](https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/dynamic/inside.html)
- **Thickness graphics.** Original charts. Each chart uses its own single linear scale for all its bars. The crust values are approximate and vary by location. The central layer’s value is the distance from the centre to its outer boundary. [USGS layer thickness estimates](https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/dynamic/inside.html)
