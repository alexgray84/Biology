Support cards

Your teacher gives you a code at the bench. Use that card. It helps with one step; the drawing and the thinking are still yours. Then do its recheck.

PCard P · Check a method step (Method steps)

Lowest lens first. Focus moving apart. Then fine focus only.

Recheck: You see only white light, no cells. Give two things to check.

OCard O · Draw what you see (Draw what you see)

Single lines, no shading, only what you see. Title and ×.

Recheck: Look again. Point to one thing you drew that you can see, and one you can’t.

WCard W · Swap the word (Wall or membrane?)

The line you see round each cell is the wall.

Recheck: Label the line round your cells. Wall or membrane? Why?

Write in your book. Check your recheck against the projected answer; your teacher will sample some. The card goes away once your recheck is right.
