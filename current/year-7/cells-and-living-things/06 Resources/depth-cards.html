Depth · Camel blood (UAE)

Camel red blood cells are oval and can swell to more than twice their size without bursting when a thirsty camel drinks a lot of water quickly. Suggest why this matters for a desert animal. What might happen to human red blood cells in the same situation?

Frame: After a long time without water … the cells take in water … because they don’t burst, they still …

Depth · Every cell has a nucleus?

Claim: “Every cell has a nucleus.” A mature xylem cell in a plant stem is a hollow tube with no nucleus and no cytoplasm. Use it to disprove the claim. Explain why being empty helps xylem, but why a nerve cell needs a nucleus.

Frame: Xylem has no ___, so the claim … Being empty helps because … A nerve cell needs a nucleus because …

Depth · Design a cell

Design a cell to carry oxygen through very narrow tubes. A: large, round, has a nucleus. B: small, flexible, thin in the middle, no nucleus. Choose, justify with two reasons, and give one disadvantage.

Frame: I choose ___ because … and … One disadvantage is …
