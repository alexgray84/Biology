Picture word bank: MRS GREN

Seven things all living things do.

🏃

Movement

Changing position: the whole organism or part of it.

Example: A plant’s leaves turn towards light.

⚡

Respiration

Releasing usable energy from food, in cells. Not breathing.

Example: Happens in every living cell, day and night.

👁️

Sensitivity

Detecting and responding to changes.

Example: You blink when something comes near your eye.

📏

Growth

A permanent increase in size.

Example: A seedling gets taller.

🐣

Reproduction

Producing offspring.

Example: A date palm makes seeds.

💨

Excretion

Removing waste made in cells.

Example: Breathing out carbon dioxide.

🍎

Nutrition

Taking in or making food.

Example: A camel eats; a plant makes its own food.

Sentence frame: “The ___, which shows ___ because ___.”
