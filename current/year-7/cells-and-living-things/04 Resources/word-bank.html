Cell structures word bank

Each structure, its job and where it is found.

Cell membrane

Controls what enters and leaves.

Found in: plant and animal cells

Cell wall

Supports the cell. Outside the membrane.

Found in: plant cells only

Large permanent vacuole

Cell sap; keeps the cell firm.

Found in: plant cells only

Nucleus

Genetic information; controls the cell.

Found in: plant and animal cells

Mitochondria

Respiration releases usable energy, day and night.

Found in: plant and animal cells

Chloroplasts

Absorb light for photosynthesis.

Found in: plant cells that get light

Cytoplasm

Where many reactions happen.

Found in: plant and animal cells
