★ Gold route

Tricky and unfamiliar cases. Judge what the evidence can and can’t show.

Aim for: Identify the structure · Justify with “because” · Judge what the evidence can and can’t show.
Extend: design a cell for a new job and explain each feature.

Question 1 · Root hair cell

A root hair cell has a long, thin projection into the soil.

One root hair is twice as long as another. Predict which absorbs water faster, and explain. What else must be the same for a fair comparison?

Question 2 · Palisade cell

A palisade cell near the top of a leaf has many chloroplasts.

Root hair cells have no chloroplasts. Using the palisade cell as a comparison, explain why that makes sense.

Question 3 · Cilia stop

In the airways, cilia stop beating, but mucus is still made.

Predict the effect, then explain why this could make lung infections more likely.
