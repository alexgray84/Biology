Specialised cells word bank

Each structure, its job and where it is found.

Root hair cell

Long projection: large surface area touching soil water, so more water is absorbed.

Found in: roots

Palisade cell

Many chloroplasts absorb lots of light for photosynthesis.

Found in: near the top of leaves

Ciliated cell

Cilia beat to move mucus and trapped dust out of the airways.

Found in: airways to the lungs

Nucleus

Every cell has one, so it can’t explain a special job.

Found in: plant and animal cells

Mitochondria

Release energy by respiration, e.g. for beating cilia.

Found in: plant and animal cells
