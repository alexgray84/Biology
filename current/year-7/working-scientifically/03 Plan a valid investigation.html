Compare the questions

A testable question names what changes and what is measured

Broad: What makes a paper helicopter fall differently?

Testable: How does the number of paper clips affect the time a paper helicopter takes to fall 2 metres?
Independent variableWhat the scientist deliberately changes.
Dependent variableWhat the scientist measures as the outcome.
Control variablesRelevant conditions kept the same because they could affect the outcome.
Explicit instruction

Change one. Measure one. Control what matters.

A control variable is not a list of everything in the room.

Ask: If this changed between tests, could it change the fall time?

Model question: How does ramp height affect the distance a toy car travels?

Teacher thinks aloud: I will measure from the bottom of the ramp to where the front of the car stops. That makes the endpoint clear.

Success criteria

The IV and DV answer the question.

Controls are relevant to fall time.

The measurement has a unit and clear endpoint.

Depth: Identify one source of random variation and explain why repeats help.
