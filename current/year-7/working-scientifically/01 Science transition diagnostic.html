Read the pattern before making a claim

Sugar was added to equal volumes of water. The same mass and stirring method were used.

Water temperature20 °C40 °C60 °C
Dissolving time118 s56 s34 s
As water temperature increased, dissolving time decreased.

This conclusion describes the visible pattern. It does not claim why the pattern occurred.
