Electric current, circuit models and symbols

“Explain electric current using circuit models and build simple circuits using the correct components/symbols.”

Big ideaA cell transfers energy to charge that is already in the wires. In a complete loop the charge flows round and comes back — it is never used up. Current is how fast the charge flows. Energy is what the components take.

AThe carrier model

  • Carriers→charge
  • Carriers per second→current
  • Gold tokens→energy
  • Loading station→cell (the source)
  • Unloading station→lamp (a component)
  • Counting gate→ammeter

Strength: it keeps charge (carriers) and energy (tokens) separate, so it shows why current is not used up. Limitation: real charge doesn’t carry lumps of energy, and it doesn’t show the lamp heating up.

BCircuit symbols

Battery = two or more cells joined. Wires are always straight ruled lines.

Knowledge organiser · AP2 point 1 · part 2 of 3

CFrom real kit to circuit diagram

  1. 1Symbols: standard symbols, never pictures
  2. 2Lines: ruled, straight, square corners
  3. 3Gaps: none — every symbol joined at both ends
  4. 4Ammeter: in the loop (in series)
  5. 5Cell: long thin line = positive (+)

DKey words and what not to confuse them with

ChargeWhat flows round the circuit. It is already in the wires and is never used up.≠ energy
CurrentThe rate of flow of charge. Measured in amps (A) with an ammeter.≠ energy or voltage
EnergyTransferred from the cell to the components (as light and by heating).≠ charge
AmmeterMeasures current. Connected in series — in the path of the loop.≠ voltmeter (connected across)
Complete circuitAn unbroken loop from one end of the cell, through the components, back to the other end.≠ wires that look close but leave a gap
ModelA simplified representation used to explain or predict. Every model has limits.≠ a picture that just looks realistic

FMisconception busters

  • ✗ “The lamp uses up the current.”✓ Current is the same all round a single loop. The lamp takes energy, not charge.
  • ✗ “The cell is full of charge that runs out.”✓ The charge is already in the wires. The cell transfers energy to it.
  • ✗ “Current comes out of both ends and clashes at the lamp.”✓ Charge flows one way round the loop, out of one end and back into the other.
  • ✗ “The ammeter goes across the lamp.”✓ That is the voltmeter. The ammeter goes in the loop, so all the charge passes through it.

GIn the UAE

Liwa desert camp: a camping torch has a cell, a switch and an LED in one loop. If sand gets into the switch the contacts no longer touch — the loop is broken, so the torch is dark even with a brand-new cell.

★ Going further: 1 amp means 1 coulomb of charge passes a point every second.

Mark scheme · 4 marks

  1. 1
    Carriers = charge; current = how many carriers pass a point each secondShows you know what current is, in the model and in real life.
  2. 1
    The same carriers (charge) come back to the cell — none are used upThis is the key point that disproves the claim.
  3. 1
    Energy tokens are loaded at the cell (source)Energy comes from the cell, not from the charge itself.
  4. 1
    Tokens are unloaded at the lamp: energy is transferred as light (and by heating)This is what the lamp really “uses” — energy.

Accept: “Electricity” is not credited unless the answer says charge or energy. Accept “the current is the same all the way round” for mark 2.

Do not accept: “The lamp uses some current.” “Current is turned into light.” “The current gets weaker.”

★ Gold extension answer: Evidence: the ammeter reads 0.25 A on both sides of the lamp. If the lamp used up current, the second reading would be lower — it isn’t.

Examiner’s eye — three answers to part (c)
0 / 4
The lamp uses up the current so less current goes back to the battery.

Repeats the misconception. No model, no energy.

2 / 4
The current isn’t used up, it is the same all the way round. The lamp gives out light because it gets energy.

Mark 2 ✓ and mark 4 ✓. No model used, current not defined, and where the energy comes from is missing.

4 / 4
The carriers stand for charge, and the current is how many carriers pass a point each second. The same carriers go all the way round and back to the cell, so the charge is not used up. Energy tokens are loaded onto the carriers at the cell. At the lamp the tokens are unloaded, so energy is transferred as light and heat — the lamp uses energy, not current.

Every marking point, in order, using both the model words and the real words.

6Exam technique for this point

  • Draw = check-list marksA 3-mark diagram is usually 3 checks: the right symbols, the meter in the right place, a complete ruled loop. Check all three before moving on.
  • Explain = linked ideasA 4-mark explain needs 4 separate ideas joined with because and so. Count your ideas against the marks.
  • Say both wordsWhen you use a model, add the real word: “carriers (charge)”, “tokens (energy)”. Examiners can then credit both.
  • Units every timeWrite 0.25 A, not 0.25. A missing unit can lose the mark.
