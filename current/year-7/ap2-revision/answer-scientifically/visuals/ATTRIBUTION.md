# Visual sources

- [Iodine stained onion cells](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Iodine_stained_onion_cells.jpg) by Anonymous140209, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The lesson reuses the 640 px crop from the Year 7 microscopy package and adapts it with tighter crops for practice and the illustrative microscope views. The crops preserve the original colours. Their sizes do not establish measured magnification or cell size.
- Microscope and friction models are original geometrical reconstructions. The microscope is an illustration of the relationship between field of view and objective choice. Photo crops are qualitative. The friction motion replays prepared illustrative readings at constant speed over the same distance for each surface. The 3D objects and force meter artwork are schematic approximations of school equipment.
- Three.js r149 is pinned from the earlier Year 7 forces package. The accompanying MIT licence is in THREE-LICENSE.txt.
- The still images are rendered from the corresponding local Three.js scenes. The microscope line diagram is reused from the existing Year 7 microscopy resources.
