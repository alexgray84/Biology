Compare a spring held stretched with a trolley speeding up. Explain why a force can change shape as well as motion.

Model answer & mark scheme · Go further

A spring can change shape while being stretched. Once held still, its opposing forces balance at the new length. A trolley with an unbalanced push changes motion. Force effects can include shape or motion.

Unmarked success criteria: answer both parts; justify the comparison using force direction, balance or effect.
