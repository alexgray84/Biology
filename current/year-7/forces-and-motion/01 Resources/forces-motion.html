Model answer & mark scheme · Go further

With a rightward resultant, a left-moving trolley initially slows. If that resultant continues, it stops, then moves right and speeds up. Distinguish the current motion from its change.

Unmarked success criteria: answer both parts; justify the comparison using force direction, balance or effect.
