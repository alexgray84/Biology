Get started

Draw two columns in your book: left and right. Place each force in its direction column.

Build understanding

Total each direction. Subtract the smaller total from the larger. State N and the direction of the larger total.

Go further

Make three nonzero forces with a resultant of 6 N right. Find a second combination and explain what stays the same.

Model answer & mark scheme · Go further

Example 1: 5 N right + 4 N right − 3 N left = 6 N right. Example 2: 8 N right + 2 N right − 4 N left = 6 N right. Each has three nonzero forces and a right total 6 N larger than its left total. Other valid combinations are accepted.

Unmarked success criteria: answer both parts; justify the comparison using force direction, balance or effect.
