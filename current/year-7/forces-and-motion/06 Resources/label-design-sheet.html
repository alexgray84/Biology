1. Generate

Sketch two different ways to organise the information for a Year 7 visitor. Include quantity, unit and place. Change how the information works, not just its colour.

2. Judge

Test both ideas: quantity and unit correct; weight place clear; visitor can explain the difference. Choose one and write a scientific reason.

3. Improve

The display will be printed in black and white. Revise your choice so meaning does not rely on colour. If it already works without colour, improve the part a new visitor could misread. Explain: I changed ____ because ____.

Model responses and success criteria are reviewed in the lesson. Several scientifically accurate designs can work.
