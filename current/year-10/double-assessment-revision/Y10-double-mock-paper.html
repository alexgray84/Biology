Section A · Multiple choice tap one answer

1Which structure is found in a bacterial cell but not in a plant cell? [1]

C. Plasmids are small rings of DNA found in bacteria. Plant cells also have a wall (cellulose), ribosomes and a membrane.

2Which statement about glycogen is correct? [1]

B. Glycogen is the animal storage carbohydrate: many glucose units. Plants store starch, not glycogen.

Section A · Multiple choice tap one answer

3Which enzyme is matched with the product it makes? [1]

B. Maltase breaks maltose into glucose. Amylase makes maltose, not amino acids (D); lipase makes fatty acids and glycerol; protease makes amino acids.

4Which cells open and close the stomata of a leaf? [1]

B. A pair of guard cells surrounds each stoma and changes shape to open or close it. Palisade cells carry out most photosynthesis.

A grower in Liwa measures the rate of photosynthesis of tomato leaves at two CO₂ concentrations, at 25 °C. Air contains about 0.04 % CO₂. Most days, the greenhouse gets at least 50 klx of sunlight.

(c)The grower can afford either extra lamps or a CO₂ supply. Evaluate which would raise the rate more. Use the graph. [6]

  1. Any 6 of these 8 points, 1 mark each
  2. 1
    Up to about 10 klx the curves are equal (6.0 a.u.): light is limiting
  3. 1
    0.04 % curve levels off at 12.0 a.u. from about 50 klx: light not limiting
  4. 1
    0.10 % curve is higher (18.0 a.u.): CO₂ was limiting on the plateau
  5. 1
    Greenhouse already gets ≥ 50 klx: extra lamps add little or nothing
  6. 1
    CO₂ raises the plateau from 12.0 to 18.0 a.u. (+50 %)
  7. 1
    Decision: buy CO₂; keep near 25 °C so enzymes are not denatured
  8. 1
    Lamps would only help at dawn, at dusk or on cloudy days
  9. 1
    Lamps cost money to run and heat the greenhouse (more cooling)

Do not accept: ‘More CO₂ is always better’ with no use of the graph.
