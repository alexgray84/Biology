Specialised-cell reference clinic

Use before the cells PSA is issued. These pages support teaching and checking prior knowledge; they are not the four assessed slides. Create your own diagrams and explanations for the official task and reference sources appropriately.

Allow extra teaching time if a chosen cell is unfamiliar. Read the features, use the diagram, then answer the short practice question before opening its review. Close the reference for a fresh oral explanation of the cell’s job and one structure–function link.

Egg cell

An animal egg cell carries one set of chromosomes and contributes cytoplasm to the zygote after fertilisation.

StructureFunction and connection
NucleusContains a haploid set of chromosomes.
CytoplasmContains molecules and organelles needed by the early embryo; the amount of stored food varies among species.
Cell membraneControls movement into and out of the cell. After fertilisation, changes help prevent entry of additional sperm.
MitochondriaSupport aerobic respiration to supply usable energy.

Practice — record in your book

An egg and a sperm both contribute genetic information. Explain one difference in their structure.

Review after your attempt

An egg has much more cytoplasm; a sperm has a streamlined shape and a flagellum for movement. Both have a haploid nucleus.

Success criteria: Names a real structural difference and relates it to contribution or movement.

Sperm cell

A sperm cell moves towards an egg and carries one set of chromosomes.

StructureFunction and connection
Head / nucleusHaploid nucleus carries genetic information.
AcrosomeContains enzymes that help penetrate the egg’s surrounding layers.
MidpieceContains many mitochondria, supporting the energy demand of movement.
Flagellum (tail)Beats to move the sperm.

Practice — record in your book

A sperm has a working nucleus but cannot beat its tail. Explain a likely functional problem.

Review after your attempt

It still carries genetic information, but movement towards the egg is impaired. Having DNA alone does not provide the movement needed to reach an egg.

Success criteria: Separates genetic contribution from the role of movement.

White blood cell: phagocyte

White blood cells have different roles. This example is a phagocyte that engulfs and digests pathogens.

StructureFunction and connection
Flexible membraneChanges shape to surround and engulf a pathogen.
CytoplasmEngulfed material is enclosed within the cell.
LysosomesContain digestive enzymes that break down engulfed material.
NucleusContains genetic information and helps control cell activities; its shape varies between types.

Practice — record in your book

Why is “all white blood cells make antibodies” an inaccurate caption for this example?

Review after your attempt

Different white blood cells have different functions. This phagocyte example engulfs and digests pathogens; antibody production belongs to particular lymphocyte-derived cells.

Success criteria: Distinguishes phagocytosis from antibody production and avoids treating all WBCs alike.

Palisade mesophyll cell

A photosynthetic leaf cell is adapted to absorb light and make organic substances through photosynthesis.

StructureFunction and connection
Many chloroplastsContain chlorophyll, increasing the capacity to absorb light.
Elongated shape / positionPalisade cells commonly lie beneath the upper leaf surface, where much light arrives.
Large permanent vacuoleCell sap supports turgor; it also leaves a thin layer of cytoplasm near the edge.
Wall, membrane and nucleusProvide support, regulate exchange and contain genetic information respectively.

Practice — record in your book

Explain why a palisade cell usually has many chloroplasts while an underground root hair cell usually does not.

Review after your attempt

Palisade cells receive light and photosynthesise, so chloroplasts serve their main role. Underground root hair cells absorb water/mineral ions and normally receive little or no light.

Success criteria: Links organelle distribution to location and function, without claiming all plant cells have chloroplasts.

Xylem vessel element

Mature vessel elements join to form a pathway carrying water and dissolved mineral ions and helping support the plant.

StructureFunction and connection
Hollow lumenMature conducting elements are dead and lack cytoplasm, leaving space for water flow.
Perforated or lost end wallsConnected elements allow water to pass along the vessel.
Lignified wallStrengthens the vessel and helps resist collapse under tension.
PitsRegions of the wall allowing lateral water movement between adjacent structures.

Practice — record in your book

A caption says a mature xylem vessel has a nucleus controlling water pumping. Correct it.

Review after your attempt

Mature conducting xylem vessel elements are dead and lack a nucleus and cytoplasm. Their hollow, connected structure allows water flow; living nuclei do not pump water along each mature element.

Success criteria: Corrects mature-cell status and connects the open lumen to transport.

Phloem sieve tube element and companion cell

Phloem transports dissolved organic substances, including sucrose, from sources to sinks. Transport directions can differ in different tubes.

StructureFunction and connection
Sieve platePerforated end wall permits movement between sieve tube elements.
Living sieve tube elementHas a thin layer of cytoplasm; at maturity it lacks a nucleus and has fewer organelles, leaving room for flow.
Companion cellAn adjacent living cell with a nucleus; it supports the sieve tube element.
Companion-cell mitochondriaSupply usable energy for processes such as active loading of sugars, depending on the plant.

Practice — record in your book

Why should a phloem diagram show a companion cell as well as the conducting tube?

Review after your attempt

The mature sieve tube element has lost its nucleus and depends on the adjacent companion cell for metabolic support; the companion cell also supports loading/unloading processes.

Success criteria: Names the distinct cells and their functional relationship; does not call the sieve tube dead.
