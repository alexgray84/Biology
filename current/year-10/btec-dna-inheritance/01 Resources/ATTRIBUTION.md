# DNA, genes and chromosomes: lesson assets

Created 29 September 2026 for Year 10 and Year 11 BTEC Applied Science, Week 5, Lesson 1.

- Frame: local copy of Arcadia Lesson Frame 1.0.0 (approved 13 September 2026) with the Year 10 Triple Biology Week 5 engine. The only engine change is that the title kicker reads the lesson's `course` label.
- Three.js model (original geometry, not photographs or scans; positions, sizes and colours are schematic; drag or arrow-key rotation, no autoplay; local SVG fallback if WebGL is unavailable):
  - `dna-model.js` (`dna`): a zoom from cell to nucleus, chromosome and DNA double helix; a 10-base-pair helix with a hidden partner strand, a highlighted gene and a single-base mutation.
- SVG diagrams in `lesson-diagrams.js` are original programmatic educational diagrams: `dna-zoom` and `dna-ladder`. Not to scale.
- Three.js 0.160.1: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r160 · MIT. The full licence is in THREE-LICENSE.txt.
- Curriculum: Pearson BTEC International Level 2 Qualifications in Applied Science, Issue 2, December 2023. Unit 1 Principles of Science, Learning aim A2 (DNA structure, genes, chromosomes, beneficial and harmful mutations), criterion A.P2. School Scheme of Work cell E6.
- All questions are original. Marks are classroom allocations, not Pearson mark schemes. This lesson is classroom practice, not Pearson Set Assignment evidence.
- UAE context: the Emirati Reference Genome Programme page on the Department of Health – Abu Dhabi website (doh.gov.ae), checked 29 September 2026. Tasks are hypothetical classroom scenarios.
- Prepared AI-style claims are authored classroom checking tasks, not transcripts or live model outputs.
- No pupil data is stored or transmitted. The pages make no network calls and keep no pupil responses.
