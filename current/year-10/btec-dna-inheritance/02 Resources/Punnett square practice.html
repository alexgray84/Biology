Alleles and Punnett squares: practice

Year 10 BTEC Applied Science · Unit 1 Principles of Science · Learning aim A2 · A.P2, A.M2

These are original practice questions. The marks are classroom allocations. This is classroom practice, not Pearson Set Assignment evidence. Write your answers in your book, then check with Show / hide answers.

Part A · Key wordseveryone

  1. Match each word to its meaning: allele, genotype, phenotype, homozygous, heterozygous. [5]

    • (a) Two identical alleles
    • (b) The characteristic that shows
    • (c) A version of a gene
    • (d) Two different alleles
    • (e) The alleles an organism has

    (a) homozygous (b) phenotype (c) allele (d) heterozygous (e) genotype [1] each.

  2. Y (yellow seeds) is dominant to y (green seeds). Give the phenotype of each genotype: YY, Yy, yy. [3]

    YY yellow [1]; Yy yellow [1]; yy green [1].

  3. Explain why a plant with genotype Yy has yellow seeds. [2]

    Y is dominant [1], so one copy is enough for its effect to show [1].

Part B · Punnett squarescore

For each cross: draw the 2 × 2 square, then give the genotype ratio, the phenotype ratio and a percentage.

  1. Yy × Yy [4]

    Gametes Y, y × Y, y [1]. Squares YY, Yy, Yy, yy [1]. 1 YY : 2 Yy : 1 yy [1]. 3 yellow : 1 green; 75% yellow [1].

  2. Yy × yy [3]

    Squares Yy, yy, Yy, yy [1]. 1 Yy : 1 yy [1]. 1 yellow : 1 green; 50% green [1].

  3. YY × yy [2]

    All Yy [1]; 100% yellow [1].

  4. Cystic fibrosis is caused by a recessive allele (f). Two carriers (Ff) have a child. Give the chance the child (a) has cystic fibrosis, (b) is a carrier. [3]

    Square: FF, Ff, Ff, ff [1]. (a) 25% [1]. (b) 50% [1].

Part C · Apply and stretchstronger answers

  1. A yellow pea plant crossed with a green plant (yy) gives some green offspring. What is the yellow parent’s genotype? Explain with a Punnett square. [3]

    Yy [1]. YY × yy gives only Yy, so no green [1]. Yy × yy gives 50% yy, so green appears [1].

  2. Two carriers have four children. A pupil says exactly one child will have cystic fibrosis. Evaluate this claim. [3]

    Each child has a 25% chance [1]. Each birth is a separate chance event [1], so the family could have none, one or more affected children [1].

  3. A cross gives 290 yellow and 110 green seeds. Does this support a 3 : 1 ratio? Show your working. [2]

    Total 400; expected 300 : 100 [1]. 290 : 110 is close, so it supports 3 : 1 [1].

Part D · Homeworkpractice

  1. HomeworkHuntington’s disease is caused by a dominant allele (H). One parent is Hh; the other is hh. What is the chance a child inherits Huntington’s disease? [3]

    Square: Hh, hh, Hh, hh [1]. 1 Hh : 1 hh [1]. 50% [1].

  2. HomeworkExplain why the genotype ratio from Yy × Yy (1 : 2 : 1) is different from the phenotype ratio (3 : 1). [2]

    YY and Yy have the same phenotype [1] because Y is dominant [1].
