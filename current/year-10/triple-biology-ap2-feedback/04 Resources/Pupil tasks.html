Model answer & mark scheme

Test pH 5 and pH 7. These give smaller intervals on either side of pH 6, where the highest rate was measured, so the location of the maximum can be found more precisely.

Mark scheme

  • Two sensible additional values either side of pH 6, within the tested gaps 4–6 and 6–8; for example 5 and 7, or 5.5 and 6.5.
  • Smaller pH intervals around the highest measured rate refine the position of the maximum / locate the optimum more precisely.

The pH pair need not be exactly 5 and 7. Credit suitable values on opposite sides of 6 within 4–8 that are not already in the table. Do not credit all enzymes work best at pH 7. The highest tested value at pH 6 does not establish an exact optimum.

A2. Explain the lower rate 2 marks

Reviewed question pack

The enzyme releases 12.0 cm³ of oxygen per minute at pH 6 and 1.0 cm³ per minute at pH 10.

(b) Explain why the rate at pH 10 is lower than the rate at pH 6. (2)

(b) Explain why the rate at pH 10 is lower than the rate at pH 6.

Model answer & mark scheme

The change in pH alters the shape of the active site. Fewer substrate molecules can bind, so fewer enzyme–substrate complexes form and the reaction is slower.

Mark scheme

  • pH changes the shape of the enzyme active site / causes denaturation affecting its shape.
  • Substrate fits less well / fewer enzyme–substrate complexes form, so the reaction rate is lower.

Do not credit enzymes die. Naming denaturation without conveying an active-site change does not independently earn two marks.

B. Food energy per gram 3 marks

Original exam-style practice

Burning 0.80 g of food raises 40 g of water by 15 °C. Use 4.2 J/(g °C).

(a) Calculate the energy transferred to the water per gram of food burned, in J/g. (2)

(b) Explain why this value can be lower than the energy released per gram by the food. (1)

(a) Calculate the energy transferred to the water per gram of food burned, in J/g.

Model answer & mark scheme

40 × 4.2 × 15 = 2520 J. 2520 ÷ 0.80 = 3150 J/g.

Mark scheme

  • Correct energy transferred to water: 2520 J.
  • 3150 J/g.

Allow consistent error carried forward for division by 0.80.

(b) Explain why this value can be lower than the energy released per gram by the food.

Model answer & mark scheme

Some released energy heats the air or container, so it does not all raise the water temperature measured.

Mark scheme

  • Identifies where released energy goes instead of heating the water.
