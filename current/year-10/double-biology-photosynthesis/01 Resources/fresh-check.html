6 minutes · alone

A green bean seedling grows in light with water and carbon dioxide available.

(a) Name the two substances used in photosynthesis and the two made. [2]

(b) Correct 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 3O₂. State the number of O atoms on the right before and after your correction. [3]

(c) Correct: “Sunlight becomes the atoms of glucose, so this seedling supplies food to a caterpillar.” Keep the valid food-supply idea. [3]

Model answer & criteria · after collection / teacher instruction

(a) [2] Uses carbon dioxide and water [1]; makes glucose and oxygen [1].

(b) [3] 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂. Replace 3O₂ with 6O₂, keeping formulae [1]. Before: right O = 6 + 6 = 12 [1]. After: 6 + 12 = 18 [1].

(c) [3] Chlorophyll absorbs light energy [1]. It becomes chemical energy in glucose; atoms come from reactants, not light [1]. Caterpillar obtains food/chemical energy by eating the plant [1].
