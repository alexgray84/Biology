Q8 · Original exam-style question · 8 marks

Write in your book. Attempt first; open a review only when your teacher asks.

12 minutes · independent practice

A green school plant is in light with water and carbon dioxide.

(a) Write the word equation for photosynthesis. [2]

(b) Write the balanced symbol equation. Show total oxygen atoms on each side. [3]

(c) Explain how photosynthesis transfers energy, and why it matters to an animal that eats this plant. [3]

Model answer & criteria · after collection / teacher instruction

(a) [2] carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen. Reactants correct [1]; products correct with reaction arrow [1]. Light/chlorophyll may be over the arrow, not in the reactant list.

(b) [3] 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂. Correct formulae and sides [1]; coefficients 6,6,1,6 [1]; oxygen 18 each side (12+6; 6+12) [1]. Count mark may credit accurate counting of their written equation; balanced-coefficient mark needs the correct equation.

(c) [3] Light energy absorbed by chlorophyll [1]; transferred to chemical energy stored in glucose [1]; animal obtains plant food/chemical energy by eating it [1]. Respiration is a valid extension but is not required.

Matter · 2 marks Correct reactants [1] and products/arrow [1].

Equation · 3 marks Formulae/sides [1]; coefficients [1]; O totals [1].

Energy · 3 marks Absorption by chlorophyll [1]; chemical energy in glucose [1]; feeding link [1].
