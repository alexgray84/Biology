Pondweed practical: does more light mean more photosynthesis?

Name Date

Aim: to find out how light intensity affects the rate of photosynthesis in pondweed.

Safety: the lamp gets hot, so don't touch the bulb. Keep water away from the lamp and plug. Dry wet hands before touching anything electrical. Wipe up spills.
Progress check 1 · before you switch on. Fill in:  C (I change) ________________   M1 (I measure) ________________   One S (I keep the same) ________________ by ________________

Method

  1. Fill a boiling tube with the sodium hydrogencarbonate solution provided. It supplies carbon dioxide.
  2. Cut a 5 cm piece of pondweed. Put it in the tube with the cut end at the top.
  3. Stand the tube in the rack. Put the beaker of water between the lamp and the tube. Turn the room lights off.
  4. Use the ruler to place the lamp 10 cm from the pondweed. Record the water temperature.
  5. Wait 2 minutes for the pondweed to adjust. Then count the bubbles for 1 minute.
  6. Repeat the 1-minute count twice more at this distance.
  7. Move the lamp to 20, 30, 40 and 50 cm. Repeat steps 4–6 at each distance.
