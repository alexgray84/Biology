# Biological molecules and food tests: lesson assets

Created 28 September 2026 for Year 10 Triple Biology, Week 5, Lesson 1.

- Frame: local copy of Arcadia Lesson Frame 1.0.0, approved 13 September 2026. Canonical template assets are unchanged.
- Three.js models (original geometry, not photographs or scans; positions, sizes and colours are schematic; drag or arrow-key rotation, no autoplay; each has a local SVG fallback if WebGL is unavailable):
  - `molecule-model.js` (`molecules`): starch, glycogen, protein and lipid built from glucose units, amino acids, and glycerol with three fatty acids. The molecules can be split into their building blocks and show element badges.
  - `foodtest-model.js` (`foodtests`): a rack of five test tubes for the iodine, Benedict’s, Biuret and ethanol-emulsion tests. It has known, mystery and glucose-series sample sets and a distilled-water control.
- SVG diagrams in `lesson-diagrams.js` are original programmatic educational diagrams: `elements`, `moleculesFallback`, `carbs`, `proteinLipid`, `testMethod`, `foodTestsFallback`, `benedictScale`, `benedictResults` and `stations`. Molecule sizes, unit counts and colours are schematic. Glucose percentages and tube colours are labelled illustrative and are not experimental measurements. The pupil resource pages reuse copies of these diagrams.
- Three.js 0.160.1: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r160 · MIT. Pinned browser build obtained from https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@0.160.1/build/three.min.js . The full licence is in THREE-LICENSE.txt.
- Fonts: optional Google Fonts links inherited from the approved frame; local Georgia/system fallbacks are provided. The resource pages use system fonts only.
- Curriculum: Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Biology (Modular) 4XBI1, Issue 2, September 2024. Specification points 2.7, 2.8, 2.9 (practical: Core Practical 1) and 2.25 (carbohydrate, protein and lipid functions only), as shown beside the outcomes.
- The Hodder/Pearson core practical lab book was used only as a method reference for Core Practical 1. No text, questions, answers, tables or images are reproduced. All questions are original, and their marks are classroom allocations, not Pearson mark schemes.
- UAE context: the National Food Security Strategy 2051 page on the official UAE Government portal (u.ae), checked 27 September 2026. The snack-label, food-basket and date-paste tasks are hypothetical classroom scenarios.
- Prepared AI-style claims are authored classroom checking tasks, not transcripts or live model outputs.
- No pupil data is stored or transmitted. The pages make no network calls and keep no pupil responses.
- No textbook scans, publisher illustrations or third-party photographs are reproduced.
