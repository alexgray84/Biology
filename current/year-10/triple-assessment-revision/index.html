The plan puts your weakest units first and brings each one back two days later for a fresh retry — spacing beats cramming.

    • Test, don’t reread.Cover the organiser and say it from memory. Rereading feels good but sticks less.
    • Space it out.Two short sessions on different days beat one long one. The countdown does this for you.
    • Mark it honestly.Use the mark scheme word for word. If a mark point isn’t in your answer, you don’t get it.
    • Fix, then retry unaided.The retry is a different question. Getting it right without help proves you’ve learned it.
    • Draw it.Circuits, force arrows, tables and graphs earn marks. Pencil and ruler, every time.
    • Mix topics at the end.The real paper jumps between topics. In the last days, do hinge checks from several units.
