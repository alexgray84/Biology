# Net gas exchange — assets and attribution

Original classroom material, rebuilt 28 October 2026 from the confirmed Year 10 Triple Biology L4 design ("Two rates, one net"). The questions, model answers, support cards, depth cards and inline SVG diagrams were written for this lesson. The exam-style questions are original, labelled "not from an exam board", and are not Pearson questions or official mark schemes.

The diagrams are schematic. Indicator colours follow the lesson convention: yellow = more CO₂, orange-red = about air level, purple = less CO₂. Every swatch also has a word label. CO₂ is grey, O₂ teal, light yellow. All rates (budget, graphs, sunflower and *Cabomba* tables) are illustrative arbitrary units, not measured data. The illustrative tubes are labelled "Illustrative: not your results".

## Gas exchange lab (simulation)

`Gas exchange lab.html`, `gas-exchange-model.js` and `gas-exchange-lab-host.js` were built for this lesson in `work/y10-photosynthesis/l4-sim/`. Its own attribution is in `ATTRIBUTION-gas-exchange-lab.md`. Two changes were made to the lesson copy only:
- a "Return to lesson" link
- the lamp label was recentred over the shared LED panel and reworded

The model rules and parameters are unchanged. The values it gives are model output, never measurements.
