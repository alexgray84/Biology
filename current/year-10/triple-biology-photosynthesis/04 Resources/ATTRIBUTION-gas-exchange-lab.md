# Gas exchange lab: attribution

Built 28 October 2026 for Year 10 Triple Biology L4 ("What can changing carbon dioxide tell us about a plant?").

- Simulation design, model, page and Three.js geometry are original to this build. There are no downloaded meshes, textures, images or fonts. All textures (bench, leaf veins, foil crinkle, muslin weave) are drawn at runtime on canvas. The page makes no network calls and stores nothing.
- The predict-then-test approach is common good practice in science simulations. No PhET code, artwork, text or data are used or adapted.
- Three.js 0.160.1 (r160): https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r160 · MIT licence. `three.min.js` is a byte-for-byte copy of the pinned build already used in `Lessons/Year 10/2026-27_Week_08_Photosynthesis_and_plant_gas_exchange/02 Resources/` (originally from https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@0.160.1/build/three.min.js). The full licence is in `THREE-LICENSE.txt`.
- The indicator colour scale and all CO₂ values are illustrative. They are not a calibration of real hydrogencarbonate indicator, and they are labelled as simulated on the page.
- No pupil data. Predictions, reasons and explanations exist only in the open page's memory, and they are lost on reload.
