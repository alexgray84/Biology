Which card do you need?

Stuck? Pick the line that sounds like you, then open that card. The card helps you start; the reasoning stays yours. After using a card, answer its recheck question with the card closed.

  • B I wrote that plants respire only at night, or that one process happens instead of the other. Card B
  • I I am not sure which colour means more or less CO₂, or I wrote that purple means oxygen. Card I
  • K My plan lists things to keep the same, but not how or why, or it has no control or repeats. Card K
  • H I wrote “proves”, “exactly equal” or “nothing is happening”, or I ignored the control. Card H
  • Q I am not sure what the question is asking me to do. Card Q

Card B · Both processes

Card B · CO₂ budget

Both processes can happen in the same leaf. Draw the two arrow lengths for the light on your screen, then write the net. Respiration: all living cells, light and dark. Photosynthesis: needs light.

Check after you try: model answer and criteria

Using card B

Dark: no photosynthesis arrow; respiration arrow only, so net CO₂ out. Bright: the photosynthesis arrow is longer than the respiration arrow, so net CO₂ in, and respiration still happens. Dim: compare the two lengths; the net depends on both rates.

Success criteria

  • Draws a respiration arrow in every light.
  • Photosynthesis arrow only when there is light.
  • Net comes from comparing the two arrows.

Close the card first. If this is right with a reason, the card is faded.

Check after you try: model answer and criteria

Using card K

Temperature: LED at a fixed distance or a heat filter, because temperature changes both rates. Leaf: same species, similar size and age, because leaves exchange different amounts. Time: same, stated time, because the colour keeps changing. Seal: same tubes, bungs tight, because leaks change the CO₂. Control: a no-leaf tube in the light. Repeats: three tubes per light level.

Success criteria

  • Each row has how and why.
  • Control and repeats are included.

O · Order of route

My gas route is missing, in the wrong order, or goes through the cuticle or xylem.

Card O · Order of the route

Check your Tuesday route against this line only. Then rewrite your route in a different pen.

Air → open stomatal pore → air spaces → moist mesophyll surface → cell

Check after you try: model answer and criteria

Code O revision

Air → open stomatal pore → air spaces → moist mesophyll surface → cell. Not through the waxy cuticle and not up the xylem.

Success criteria

  • Every place in order.
  • Through the open pore, not the cuticle or xylem.

Y · whY that direction

My route is right, but I did not say which side has the higher concentration.

Card Y · whY that direction

Your route is right. Add the concentration comparison in a different pen.

Net diffusion goes from the higher to the lower concentration. Say which side is higher.

Check after you try: model answer and criteria

Code Y revision

“CO₂ concentration is higher in the outside air than inside the leaf, so the net diffusion is into the leaf.” The higher side must be named.

Success criteria

  • Names which side is higher.
  • Net direction from higher to lower.

J · Just named

I named a leaf feature but did not say what it changes.

Card J · Just named

Add the physical effect to one named feature, in a different pen.

Feature → what it changes physically → why that helps gas exchange or light capture.

Check after you try: model answer and criteria

Code J revision

For example: thin leaf → short diffusion distance → gases reach mesophyll cells quickly. Or: transparent epidermis → light passes through → palisade chloroplasts absorb it.

Success criteria

  • Feature named.
  • What it changes physically.
  • Why that helps gas exchange or light capture.
