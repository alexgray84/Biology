Which card do you need?

Stuck? Pick the line that sounds like you, then open that card. The card helps you start; the reasoning stays yours. After the card, close it and answer its recheck question in your book. Mark it when the recheck answers are shown.

  • B I wrote that plants respire only at night, or that one process happens instead of the other. Card B
  • Z Not sure which colour means more or less CO₂, or wrote that purple means oxygen. Card Z
  • K My plan lists things to keep the same, but not how or why, or it has no control or repeats. Card K
  • H I wrote “proves”, “exactly equal” or “nothing is happening”, or I ignored the control. Card H
  • Q I am not sure what the question is asking me to do. Card Q
