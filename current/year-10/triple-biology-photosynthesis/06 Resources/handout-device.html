(b) The grower destarched a no-magnesium seedling, left it in light for a day, then starch-tested a leaf. The yellow areas stayed orange-brown; the green areas turned blue-black. The grower says: “This shows that light is needed for photosynthesis.” Evaluate this conclusion. [2]
