# What does a plant need, and how do we know? — assets and attribution

Original classroom material, built 29 October 2026 from the confirmed Year 10 Triple Biology evidence-lesson design. The questions, model answers, support cards, depth boxes, printed handout, fresh-check slip and inline SVG diagrams were written for this lesson. The exam-style question is original, labelled "not from an exam board", and is not a Pearson question or an official mark scheme.

The diagrams are schematic. Colour conventions: CO₂ grey (drawn as O=C=O, so its shape also identifies it), O₂ teal, light yellow, leaf green. Iodine: blue-black = starch, orange-brown = no starch, always with the words. All leaf, tray and cucumber results are illustrative, not real data. The fallback values are the Lesson 2 pondweed-lab simulated values, labelled "simulated". No live bubble count is quoted before it is measured. The Al Ain greenhouse is a hypothetical context; no claim about a real farm is made.

## Reused material
