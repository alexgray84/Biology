Plain words and colour words

Plain words

Plain-English meanings for today’s key words.

destarch
keep the plant in the dark (about 48 hours) so it uses up its stored starch
variegated
a leaf with green parts and white parts; the white parts have no chlorophyll
deficiency
not enough of something the plant needs
soda lime
a solid that absorbs carbon dioxide
heat shield
a beaker of water between the lamp and the tube that absorbs heat
relative intensity
light intensity compared with the brightest position, set at 100

Colour words

Every colour has a word. Iodine solution is orange-brown. Blue-black means starch. Orange-brown means no starch. In the pondweed diagram, teal labels oxygen and grey labels carbon dioxide.
