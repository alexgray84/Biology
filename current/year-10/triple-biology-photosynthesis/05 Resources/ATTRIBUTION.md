# Planning a valid investigation — assets and attribution

Original classroom material, built 28 October 2026 from the confirmed Year 10 Triple Biology L5 design ("A plan that convinces a sceptic"). The questions, model answers, feedback-code board, support cards, depth cards and inline SVG diagrams were written for this lesson. The exam-style question is original, labelled "not from an exam board", and is not a Pearson question or an official mark scheme.

The two coded examples were written for this lesson and are not taken from any pupil's work. The flawed method is synthetic and labelled "a pupil's method, not yours". The diagrams are schematic. Indicator colours follow the unit convention: yellow = more CO₂, orange-red = about air level, purple = less CO₂, each with a word label; the indicator shows CO₂ change only and is not an O₂ test. CO₂ is grey, O₂ teal, light yellow. All pH values are illustrative, not measured data. The Dubai vertical farm is a hypothetical test; no claim about a real farm is made.
