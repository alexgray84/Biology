How codes work

Your teacher codes your work. A code names your next step; it is not a grade. Up to two codes; the circled one is today’s job. Open the card with the same letter and words, do its board activity on that answer in a different pen, then answer its recheck with the card closed.

R Both rates

What it means

Name both processes and say which is faster.

Board activity

Budget card: write P __ R (>, < or =). Rewrite: “___ is faster than ___, so overall ___.”

Two arrows, one net. Compare the two rates first.

P R   (>, < or =)

“___ is faster than ___, so overall ___.”

Do this on the coded answer, in a different pen. Keep your first attempt.

Recheck (new values)

Duckweed in dim light: P = 6, R = 6 (a.u. per hour). A pupil writes: “The plant has stopped respiring.” Correct it using both rates.

Answer in your book with the card closed. Your teacher shows the answer.

S Sign and direction

What it means

Board activity

L Limiting factor

What it means

Say light is no longer limiting and name the factor that is, using the conditions given.

Board activity

Graph strip: mark the region. Then: “Light is no longer limiting; ___ now limits photosynthesis.”

Find the flat part of the graph. Read the conditions given before you name a factor.

“Light is no longer limiting; ___ now limits photosynthesis.”

Do this on the coded answer, in a different pen. Keep your first attempt.

Recheck (new values)

Seaweed at a constant 25 °C: net O₂ production is the same at light 40 and 60. Explain why.

Answer in your book with the card closed. Your teacher shows the answer.

J Judge the claim

What it means

Match the claim to the evidence: the indicator shows CO₂ only; use the control; say how strong.

Board activity

Claim ladder: proves, supports or cannot tell. Use the control. Give one way to make it stronger.

Pick the rung that fits the evidence, then use that word.

  1. proves no other explanation is possible
  2. supports fits the evidence, with a control
  3. cannot tell the evidence cannot decide

“The evidence ___ the claim, because the control ___.”

“To make it stronger: ___.”

Do this on the coded answer, in a different pen. Keep your first attempt.

Recheck (new values)

Bright light: a leaf tube went orange-red → purple; its no-leaf control stayed orange-red; one tube. Claim, and how strong?

Answer in your book with the card closed. Your teacher shows the answer.
