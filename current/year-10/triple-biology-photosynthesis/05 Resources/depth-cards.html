Changed condition: a different lamp. Repeatable is not the same as valid.

Repeatable but wrong

A group repeats its filament-lamp experiment five times and gets the same colours every time. They say: “Our results are repeatable, so they are valid.”

  • (a) They switch to an LED and the gradient remains. Does that prove light caused it?
  • (b) Design a test that separates the effect of light from the effect of heat. Say which result would show that heat was contributing.

Optional frame

(a) Repeatable means … Heat is a … error, because … (b) Same distances, but temperature held constant by … If heat was contributing, the gradient would …

Write your answer in your book, headed with the card name. Your teacher shows the answers.

Changed constraint, UAE context. A hypothetical test with no claim about a real farm.

The ceiling

A Dubai vertical farm tests lettuce leaves under LED grow lights. A technician leaves bright and dim tubes for 18 hours; both end deep purple.

  • (a) Explain why this hides the effect of light.
  • (b) Justify a better timing plan.

Optional frame

(a) In a sealed tube, photosynthesis lowers the CO₂ until … so both tubes … (b) Better: readings every … minutes, comparing … because …

Write your answer in your book, headed with the card name. Your teacher shows the answers.
