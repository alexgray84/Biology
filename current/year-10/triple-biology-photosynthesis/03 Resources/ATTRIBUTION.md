# Leaf adaptations — assets and attribution

Original classroom material, produced 27 October 2026 for the approved Year 10 Triple Biology leaf lesson. The questions, model answers, partial scaffolds, SVG diagrams and Three.js scene geometry were authored for this lesson. Original exam-style practice is labelled as such; it is not a Pearson question or official mark scheme.

The scientific representations show a typical terrestrial broad leaf, with a separate supplied floating-leaf comparison. Colours, tissue thicknesses and organelles are illustrative and enlarged. The model shows structures and an explicitly revealed example gas route; it does not calculate or measure photosynthesis rate.
