Different evidence types answer different questions

01Knowledge evidence states accurate science.

02A practical record shows method, observations, results and changes.

03An explanation links scientific ideas with because, therefore or so that.

Worked example

Watch the decisions, not just the answer

1Statement: the cell membrane controls movement of substances.

2Practical record: labelled observation with magnification and scale.

3Explanation: a feature is linked causally to what the cell can do.
