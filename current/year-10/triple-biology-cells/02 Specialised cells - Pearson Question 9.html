How is each cell adapted?

The nerve cell called a motor neurone (page 19) and a palisade cell of a leaf (page 19) are both very specialised cells. Read about each of these and explain very briefly (three or four lines) how each is adapted to its function.

Motor neurone

Function: carries electrical impulses from the central nervous system towards a muscle.

Long axon: extends over a long distance. Electrical impulses travel along it.

Myelin sheath: insulates the axon and helps impulses travel faster.

Branched endings: allow the neurone to connect with its target cells.

Palisade cell

Function: carries out photosynthesis in a leaf.

Many chloroplasts: contain chlorophyll, which absorbs light energy.

Near the upper leaf surface: this position gives access to light entering the leaf.

Photosynthesis uses light energy to make glucose.

Write in your book: plan one relevant feature together, then write your own 3–4 lines for each cell. Explain what the feature enables and how that supports the cell's function.
