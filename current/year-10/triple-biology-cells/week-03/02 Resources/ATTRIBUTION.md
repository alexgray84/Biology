# Week 3 lesson assets

Created 13 September 2026 for the Year 10 Biology lessons.

- Frame: local copy of Arcadia Lesson Frame 1.0.0, approved 13 September 2026. Canonical template assets are unchanged.
- Cell cutaway: original Three.js geometry, not a micrograph. Positions, sizes and colours are schematic. Drag rotation has no autoplay. The local SVG remains available if WebGL is unavailable.
- All SVG diagrams: original programmatic educational diagrams. Molecule counts, dimensions and colours are illustrative, not experimental measurements.
- Three.js 0.160.1: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r160 · MIT. Pinned browser build obtained from https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@0.160.1/build/three.min.js . Full licence in THREE-LICENSE.txt.
- Fonts: optional Google Fonts links inherited from the approved frame; local Georgia/system fallbacks are provided.
- Curriculum: Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Biology 4BI1, Issue 3 September 2024. Selected components and preparation only, as shown beside the outcomes.
- Prepared AI-style claims are authored classroom checking tasks, not transcripts or live model outputs. No pupil responses are transmitted or stored.
- UAE context checked 13 September 2026 against the official government National Genome Strategy and Water Security Strategy 2036 pages. Research/growing scenarios are expressly hypothetical.
- Project Zero routines: Claim, Support, Question and What Makes You Say That?, Harvard Graduate School of Education. Prompts are original subject applications.
- No textbook scans, publisher illustrations or third-party photographs are reproduced.
