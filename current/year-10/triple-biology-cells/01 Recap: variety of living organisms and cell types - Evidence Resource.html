Classify from cell evidence

Specification 1.2Specification 1.310 marks

Learning outcomes: classify an unfamiliar organism using evidence; compare eukaryotic and prokaryotic organisation.

Evidence

Organism X is one cell with a nucleus, chloroplasts and a flagellum. Organism Y is smaller, with a cell wall, plasmids and circular DNA loose in its cytoplasm.

How to use the command words

Classify: name the group, then justify it from the stimulus. Compare: make paired statements using “whereas”; do not write two separate lists.

(3)1. Classify organism X. Justify your answer with two features.
(3)2. Classify organism Y. Justify your answer with two features.
(4)3. Compare the cell organisation of X and Y. Include one similarity and three paired differences.

Before you review

□ I named each group.
□ I quoted stimulus evidence.
□ I used paired comparisons.
□ I did not claim that unseen means absent.
