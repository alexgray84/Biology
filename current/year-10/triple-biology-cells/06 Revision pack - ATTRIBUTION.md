# Revision pack — Cells, enzymes & osmosis (Year 10 Triple Biology)

Created 21 September 2026 for the Year 10 Triple Biology Cells unit (10/Bio/Tri2).

- **Format:** single self-contained HTML file (`06 Revision pack - cells, enzymes and osmosis.html`). All CSS, JavaScript and diagrams are inline. No build step and no local asset folder are required for the pupil route.
- **Questions:** every question is **original**, authored in the Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Biology (4BI1) house style — genuine command words, realistic 7–10 mark allocations, sub-parts and Edexcel-convention mark schemes. **They are not reproductions of copyrighted past-paper questions**, and no exam-board mark scheme is reproduced. Questions are labelled "Original · Edexcel IGCSE style" on the pack.
- **Diagrams and graphs:** all SVGs (bacterial cell, animal cell, specialised cells — sperm/red blood/root hair, stem-cell differentiation flow, magnification triangle, mm/µm/nm unit ladder, biological-molecule building blocks, food-test result tubes, enzyme rate–temperature graph, enzyme rate–pH graph, diffusion, active transport, surface-area:volume cubes, osmosis membrane, osmosis results graph) are original programmatic educational schematics. Labels, dimensions, molecule counts and plotted values are illustrative for teaching, not experimental measurements or scans.
- **Practical data (Table 1 / Fig 5):** the potato-osmosis results are illustrative example data constructed so the percentage-change calculation and the zero-crossing interpretation work cleanly. They are not from a specific published dataset.
- **Fonts:** optional Google Fonts (Fraunces, Montserrat) via the same links used by the approved lesson frame; Georgia/system fallbacks apply if the network is unavailable (as in the offline sandbox).
- **Curriculum reference:** Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Biology 4BI1, Issue 3 (September 2024). Thirteen spec-point tabs, some strands sensibly combined:
  - 1(b) structure of bacterial cells
  - 2(a) cell differentiation & specialised cells; 2(a) stem cells in medicine (advantages/disadvantages)
  - 2(b) animal cell organelles; 2(b) magnification & unit conversions
  - 2(c) biological molecules (2.7–2.8); food tests (2.9); enzymes & temperature (2.10–2.12); enzymes & pH (2.13–2.14B)
  - 2(d) diffusion & active transport (2.15, + diffusion practical from 2.17); factors affecting rate incl. surface area : volume (2.16); osmosis (2.15); the required practical investigating osmosis in plant tissue (2.17)
  - The 2(a) tabs are coded to the Levels-of-organisation section; adjust the codes if the school scheme numbers differentiation/stem cells differently.
- No textbook scans, publisher illustrations or third-party photographs are reproduced. No pupil names, class data or private companion links are included.
