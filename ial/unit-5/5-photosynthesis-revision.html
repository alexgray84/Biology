10 Describe the light-dependent reactions of photosynthesis. In your answer, include the role of chlorophyll, electron transport, photolysis, and the difference between cyclic and non-cyclic photophosphorylation. (8)
Examiner advice: 8-mark questions need detail and structure. Plan your answer: start with what happens to light energy, then follow the electrons step by step. Don't forget to distinguish cyclic from non-cyclic.
Planning scaffold
Organise your answer into these sections:

2. Excited electrons → "The electrons become excited / raised to a higher energy level and..."
3. Non-cyclic pathway → "In non-cyclic photophosphorylation, electrons pass from PSII to PSI along..."
4. Photolysis → "The electrons lost from PSII are replaced by..."
5. Products → "The products of the light-dependent reactions are..."

Write your answer here...
Key points to hit: (1) chlorophyll absorbs light → electrons excited, (2) electrons pass along a chain of electron carriers, (3) energy from electrons used to make ATP by chemiosmosis, (4) in non-cyclic: electrons from PSII → PSI → reduce NADP, (5) photolysis replaces electrons lost from PSII: H₂O → 2H⁺ + 2e⁻ + ½O₂, (6) in cyclic: electrons from PSI return to PSI — only ATP produced, no NADP reduced, no photolysis.
Award 1 mark each for any eight of:
• Light energy is absorbed by chlorophyll (in photosystems / PSII and PSI) (1)
• Electrons in chlorophyll become excited / raised to a higher energy level (1)
• Excited electrons leave the chlorophyll molecule (1)
• Electrons pass along a chain of electron carriers / electron transport chain (1)
• Energy released from electrons is used to produce ATP / photophosphorylation (1)
Accept: energy used to phosphorylate ADP to ATP / chemiosmosis
• Non-cyclic: electrons pass from PSII → electron carriers → PSI (1)
• Electrons from PSI (along with H⁺) reduce NADP to reduced NADP / NADPH (1)
• Photolysis of water replaces electrons lost from PSII: H₂O → 2H⁺ + 2e⁻ + ½O₂ (1)
• Oxygen is released as a by-product of photolysis (1)
• Cyclic: electrons from PSI return to PSI (via electron carriers) (1)
• In cyclic, only ATP is produced / no reduced NADP / no photolysis / no O₂ released (1)

Key Knowledge — Photosynthesis (5.1–5.7)

5.1 — The Overall Reaction

6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
↕
Light energy splits strong bonds in H₂O → hydrogen stored in glucose by combining with CO₂ → O₂ released

5.2 — ATP: The Energy Currency

Photophosphorylation: ADP + Pi → ATP (requires energy input from light)

Hydrolysis: ATP → ADP + Pi (releases energy for immediate use in biological processes)

5.3 — Light-Dependent Reactions

Location: Thylakoid membranes

Light absorbed by chlorophyll → electrons excited
↓
Electrons pass along carrier chain → ATP produced
↓
Non-cyclic: e⁻ from PSII → PSI → reduce NADP
↓
Photolysis: H₂O → 2H⁺ + 2e⁻ + ½O₂

Cyclic: Only PSI. Electrons return to PSI. Only ATP made. No NADP reduced. No photolysis.

5.4 — Light-Independent (Calvin Cycle)

Location: Stroma

CO₂ + RuBP (5C) → 2× GP (3C) [RUBISCO]
↓ reduced NADP + ATP
GP reduced → GALP (3C)
↓
Some GALP → glucose / other molecules
↓ ATP
Most GALP → regenerate RuBP

5.5 — Chloroplast Structure

Double membrane (envelope)
Controls substances entering/leaving
Thylakoids (in grana)
Large SA for light-dependent reactions / pigments / ETC
Stroma
Fluid matrix — enzymes for Calvin cycle, DNA, ribosomes
Intergranal lamellae
Connect grana — allow transport between stacks

5.6 & 5.7 — Pigments

Absorption spectrum
Graph of wavelengths absorbed by a pigment
Action spectrum
Graph of rate of photosynthesis at each wavelength
Chromatography
Separates pigments based on solubility in solvent
Rf value
= distance by pigment ÷ distance by solvent. Identifies pigments.
