Part B — Core Practical 7: Plant Tissue Microscopy

Q12 marks
State why toluidine blue O is described as a metachromatic stain.
Q24 marks
A student prepares a TS of a plant stem stained with toluidine blue O. The whole section appears uniformly dark blue with no colour differentiation between tissues.
Explain why the student cannot distinguish between the tissues, and suggest how they could improve their preparation.
Q36 marks
A student's plan diagram of a stem TS contains these errors: individual cells drawn in the xylem region, vascular bundles drawn as circles rather than their actual shape, shading used to show sclerenchyma, and no labels present.
Evaluate the student's plan diagram. For each error, explain why it is incorrect and state how it should be corrected.

Part E — Timed Exam Practice

Time allowed: 20 minutes. Write under exam conditions.

Q1012 marks
A group of students investigated whether plant extracts could be used as natural preservatives for food. They tested extracts from garlic, rosemary, and thyme against bacteria commonly found on food surfaces.

They prepared their extracts by grinding each plant material with a pestle and mortar, adding 10 cm³ of ethanol, and filtering the mixture. They soaked filter paper discs in each extract for 5 minutes, then placed them on agar plates seeded with bacteria. The plates were incubated at 25°C for 48 hours.
(a) Explain two aseptic techniques the students should use when setting up the agar plates, giving a reason for each. (4)

(b) Identify one control variable and explain why it must be kept constant. (2)

(c) The diameter of the clear zone around the garlic disc was 22 mm. Calculate the area of the zone of inhibition. Give your answer to the nearest whole number. (2)

(d) The zones were not perfectly circular. Suggest one reason and describe how to obtain a more reliable measurement. (2)

(e) Evaluate the conclusion that "garlic is the most effective antimicrobial agent tested." (2)
